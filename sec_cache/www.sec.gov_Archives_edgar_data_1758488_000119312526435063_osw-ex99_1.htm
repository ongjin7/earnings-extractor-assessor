OneSpaWorld Reports Record Third Quarter Fiscal 2026 Results

Total Revenues of $276.5 Million, Net Income of $28.2 Million and Adjusted EBITDA of $39.2 Million

Increases Fiscal 2026 Guidance to $1.033 to $1.038 Billion in Total Revenues and $138.0 to $140.0 Million in Adjusted EBITDA

Board Approves a New $100 Million Share Repurchase Program and a 25% Increase in Quarterly Dividend to $0.0625 Per Share

 

Nassau, Bahamas, October 28, 2026 - OneSpaWorld Holdings Limited (NASDAQ: OSW) (“OneSpaWorld,” or the “Company”), the pre-eminent global provider of health, wellness and aesthetic services, products and experiences for guests on-board cruise ships and in destination resorts around the world, today announced its financial results for the third quarter and first nine months of fiscal 2026 ended September 30, 2026.

 

Leonard Fluxman, Executive Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, commented: “We delivered excellent third quarter performance, reporting results that surpassed our guidance and marking our 22nd consecutive record quarter of year-over-year growth in Total revenues and Adjusted EBITDA. Our sustained momentum reflects the durability of our business model, the successful execution of our strategy and our unwavering commitment to deliver memorable experiences for guests of our health and wellness centers at sea and on land. Our Board’s approval of a new $100 million share repurchase program, together with a 25% increase in our quarterly dividend, demonstrates the power of our asset-light operations to generate consistently strong and growing free cash flow; enabling us to create significant value for our shareholders. I want to thank our outstanding global team for their continuing commitment to delivering our strong performance.”

 

Mr. Fluxman continued: “Our performance was broad-based, with gains across all key operating metrics, most notably maritime pre-booking revenue, average guest spend, service frequency and guest penetration. We also set the stage for further expansion by entering into new agreements with Azamara Cruises and Silversea Cruises. These agreements extend our long-term partnerships with these brands and enable us to enhance and elevate spa facilities to broaden and innovate the range of services, products and experiences we offer to cruise line guests. We begin the final quarter of the year strongly positioned and expect the focused execution of our strategy to give us our fourth consecutive year of record results in fiscal 2026.”

 

Stephen Lazarus, President, Chief Financial Officer and Chief Operating Officer, added: “I am pleased to report continued strength in the third quarter, which saw Total revenues increase 7% and Adjusted EBITDA increase 12%. Ongoing innovation in our guest services and products and our collaboration with our cruise line and destination resort partners serve to further bolster our competitive position and drive our consistent growth. We also continued to implement and integrate AI technology to drive operational efficiencies and profitable growth.”

 

Mr. Lazarus noted further, “We continued to execute our balanced capital allocation strategy, leveraging our strong cash flow generation and our positive outlook, to return $42.3 million to shareholders in the third quarter, including $37.2 million attributable to the repurchase of approximately 1.4 million common shares and $5.1 million via our quarterly dividend. In addition, we repaid $11.3 million of our Term Loan Facility in the third quarter. We ended the quarter with $32.1 million in cash, $80.9 million in total liquidity, and Total debt, net of deferred financing costs, of $70.5 million. Since initiating our share repurchase program in April 2025, we have repurchased approximately 3.2 million common shares, completing the authorized repurchase of $75 million of our common shares.”

 

Mr. Lazarus concluded, “With our strong year-to-date performance and continuing momentum into the fourth quarter, we have raised our fiscal year 2026 guidance. We now expect fiscal 2026 Total revenues to increase 10% and Adjusted EBITDA to increase 13% at the midpoints of our guidance ranges, compared with actual fiscal 2025 results, excluding exited and reorganized operations. Additionally, our Board has approved a new $100 million share repurchase program and a 25% increase to our quarterly dividend.”

About OneSpaWorld

 

Headquartered in Nassau, Bahamas, OneSpaWorld is one of the largest health and wellness services companies in the world. OneSpaWorld’s distinguished health and wellness centers offer guests a comprehensive suite of premium health, wellness, aesthetics and fitness services, treatments, and products, currently on-board 208 cruise ships and at 12 destination resorts around the world. OneSpaWorld holds the leading market position within the cruise industry segment of the international leisure market, which it has earned over six decades based on its exceptional service; expansive global recruitment, training and logistics platforms; irreplicable operating infrastructure; powerful team; and product innovation, delivering tens of millions of extraordinary guest experiences and outstanding service to its cruise line and destination resort partners.

Note Regarding Non-GAAP Financial Information

 

This press release includes financial measures that are not calculated in accordance with GAAP, including Adjusted net income, Adjusted net income per diluted share and Adjusted EBITDA.

 

We define Adjusted net income as Net income, adjusted for items, including Amortization of intangible assets; Long-lived assets impairment; and Stock-based compensation. Adjusted net income per diluted share is defined as Adjusted net income divided by Diluted weighted average shares outstanding during the period, as if such shares had been outstanding during the entire three and nine month periods ended September 30, 2026 and 2025.

 

We define Adjusted EBITDA as Net income adjusted for items, including Income tax expense; Interest expense, net; Depreciation and amortization; Long-lived assets impairment; and Stock-based compensation as set forth below.

 

We believe that these non-GAAP measures, when reviewed in conjunction with GAAP financial measures, and not in isolation or as substitutes for analysis of our results of operations under GAAP, are useful to investors as they are widely used measures of performance and the adjustments we make to these non-GAAP measures provide investors further insight into our profitability and additional perspectives in comparing our performance to other companies and in comparing our performance over time on a consistent basis.

 

These non-GAAP measures have limitations as profitability measures. Adjusted EBITDA does not include total amounts for interest expense, net on our debt and provision for income taxes, and does not reflect the effect of our expenditures for capital assets and certain intangible assets. Adjusted net income and Adjusted net income per diluted share do not reflect the effect of our expenditures for certain intangible assets. In addition, all of these non-GAAP measures do not include the effect of non-cash stock-based compensation expense or non-cash long-lived assets impairment charges. Because of these limitations, the Company relies primarily on its GAAP results.

 

In the future, we may incur expenses similar to those for which adjustments are made in calculating these non-GAAP measures. Our presentation of Adjusted net income, Adjusted net income per diluted share and Adjusted EBITDA should not be construed as a basis to infer that our future results will be unaffected by extraordinary, unusual, or nonrecurring items.
