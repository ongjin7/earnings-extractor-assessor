EXHIBIT 99.1

Midland States Bancorp, Inc. Announces 2026 Third Quarter Results
Effingham, IL, October 22, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Midland States Bancorp, Inc. (Nasdaq: MSBI) (the “Company”) today reported net income available to common shareholders of $20.6 million, or $0.95 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2026, compared to net income available to common shareholders of $17.7 million, or $0.82 per diluted share, for the second quarter of 2026. This also compares to net income available to common shareholders of $5.3 million, or $0.24 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2025.

2026 Third Quarter Results

•Net income available to common shareholders of $20.6 million, or $0.95 per diluted share
•Return on average assets of 1.35% and return on average tangible common equity of 18.50%
•Net interest margin of 4.03% compared to 3.98% in the prior quarter; current quarter included a $1.6 million interest recovery on the payoff of a nonaccrual loan
•Total loans increased $39.1 million, including $23.7 million of net growth in the Community Bank loan portfolio, or 2.8% annualized, during the quarter
•Total capital to risk-weighted assets of 15.60% and common equity tier 1 capital of 10.36%
•Ratio of nonperforming assets to total assets of 0.86%, a decrease of five basis points compared to prior quarter
Discussion of Outlook; President & Chief Executive Officer, Jeffrey G. Ludwig:
“Our third quarter results reflect another solid quarter of execution and continued progress toward building a higher-performing community bank. We delivered diluted earnings per share of $0.95 and a 1.35% return on average assets while maintaining strong net interest margin. The Community Bank generated loan growth, while we continued the planned runoff of non-core portfolios, further improving the composition of our balance sheet.

"We also made meaningful progress resolving legacy credit issues. Past-due, substandard and nonperforming loans declined, net charge-offs moderated from the prior quarter, and our nonperforming asset ratio improved to 0.86%. We remain disciplined in addressing the remaining legacy credits while supporting high-quality loan growth in our markets.

"Strong earnings continued to support our capital position. We enter the fourth quarter with solid momentum and remain focused on profitable relationship growth, continued credit normalization and disciplined capital deployment to support long-term shareholder value.”

Key Points for Third Quarter and Outlook
Growth Trends in Community Bank & Wealth Management

•Total loans at September 30, 2026 were $4.28 billion, an increase of $39.1 million from June 30, 2026. Key changes in the loan portfolio were as follows:

◦Community Bank balances increased $23.7 million, or 2.8% annualized.

◦Specialty finance loans increased $28.0 million to $560.1 million from June 30, 2026.

◦Non-core loans, which include our third-party lending and servicing programs and remaining equipment finance portfolio, decreased $12.7 million to $295.7 million from June 30, 2026.

•Total deposits were $5.62 billion at September 30, 2026, a decrease of $89.8 million from June 30, 2026. Key changes in deposits were as follows:

◦Retail deposits decreased $112.7 million, primarily reflecting $115.7 million of balance reductions resulting from actions taken to manage liquidity and deposit pricing.

◦Commercial and public funds deposits increased $35.7 million and $33.7 million, respectively.

◦Servicing and higher-cost brokered deposits decreased $26.5 million and $7.9 million, respectively.

•Wealth Management revenue totaled $8.7 million in the third quarter of 2026. Assets under administration remained stable at $4.77 billion at September 30, 2026, compared to $4.78 billion at June 30, 2026.

Net Interest Margin

•Net interest margin increased five basis points to 4.03% in the third quarter of 2026, benefiting from a $1.6 million interest recovery on the payoff of a nonaccrual loan and continued balance sheet and interest rate risk management, partially offset by higher funding costs. The cost of deposits increased four basis points to 1.82%.

Trends in Noninterest Income and Expense
•Noninterest income was $23.2 million for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $23.8 million for the second quarter of 2026.

•Noninterest expense was $52.8 million for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $50.8 million for the second quarter of 2026, driven by an increase in variable compensation expense.

•Income tax expense was $6.6 million, resulting in an effective tax rate of 22.4% for the third quarter of 2026 compared to 22.9% and 33.2% for the second quarter of 2026 and third quarter of 2025, respectively. We currently expect our effective tax rate to be approximately 23% for the full year, subject to changes in earnings mix, state tax legislation, and other factors.

Continued Progress on Credit Quality
•Loans 30-89 days past due decreased to $9.1 million, or 0.21% of total loans, at September 30, 2026, compared to $11.0 million, or 0.26% of total loans, at June 30, 2026. Substandard accruing loans decreased by $0.4 million to $71.2 million at September 30, 2026.

•Nonperforming loans decreased to $58.7 million, or 1.37% of total loans, at September 30, 2026, compared to $60.9 million, or 1.43% of total loans, at June 30, 2026.

•Net charge-offs were $5.4 million for the third quarter of 2026, including $3.2 million of charge-offs on two previously identified nonperforming commercial real estate relationships in the Community Bank portfolio, partially offset by a $0.8 million recovery on the payoff of another previously identified nonperforming commercial real estate relationship.

•Provision for credit losses on loans was $3.7 million for the third quarter of 2026, reflecting improved credit quality metrics, including favorable past due and delinquency trends.

•Allowance for credit losses on loans was $60.9 million, or 1.42% of total loans, at September 30, 2026, compared to an allowance of $62.5 million, or 1.47% of total loans, at June 30, 2026.

About Midland States Bancorp, Inc.
Midland States Bancorp, Inc. is a community-based financial holding company headquartered in Effingham, Illinois, and is the sole shareholder of Midland States Bank. As of September 30, 2026, the Company had total assets of approximately $6.82 billion, and its Wealth Management Group had assets under administration of approximately $4.77 billion. The Company provides a full range of commercial and consumer banking products and services, merchant credit card services, trust and investment management, insurance and financial planning services. For additional information, visit https://www.midlandsb.com/ or https://www.linkedin.com/company/midland-states-bank.
