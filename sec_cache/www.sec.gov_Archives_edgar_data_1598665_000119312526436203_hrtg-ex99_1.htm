Heritage Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

Tampa, FL – October 28, 2026: Heritage Insurance Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: HRTG) (“Heritage” or the “Company”), a super-regional property and casualty insurance holding company, today reported third quarter of 2026 financial results.

Third Quarter 2026 Result Highlights

•
Year-to-date net income of $149.3 million, up 15.8%, and earnings per share of $4.94 per diluted share, up 18.5%, compared with $128.9 million and $4.17 per diluted share, for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.
•
Third quarter net income of $51.1 million, compared with $50.4 million in the prior-year quarter, despite $15.8 million of pre-tax catastrophe losses in the current quarter, compared with no catastrophe losses in the prior-year quarter.
•
Earnings per share increased 5.5% to $1.72 per diluted share in the third quarter, as compared to $1.63 per diluted share in the prior-year quarter, aided by a 4.0% reduction in diluted shares outstanding.
•
Total revenue increased 3.2% to $219.2 million from $212.5 million in the prior-year quarter, with net premiums earned up 5.4% and net investment income up 17.3% from the prior-year quarter.
•
Net loss ratio increased 1.3 percentage points to 39.6%, from 38.3% in the prior-year quarter, reflecting higher catastrophe and weather losses, largely offset by $14.0 million of favorable prior-year reserve development.
•
Net combined ratio was relatively flat at 73.0%, compared with 72.9% in the prior-year quarter.
•
Net expense ratio improved 1.2 percentage points to 33.4%, from 34.6% in the prior-year quarter.
•
Annualized return on equity of 35.1% for the third quarter of 2026 and 36.1% year to date, with stockholders’ equity at September 30, 2026 up 37.0% from September 30, 2025.
•
Book value per share of $20.22, up 23.4% from year-end 2025 and 42.9% from the prior-year quarter.
•
Debt-to-capital ratio improved to 10.5% from 15.3% in the prior-year quarter.
•
Repurchased 1,101,508 shares of common stock at a cost of $27.9 million in 2026.

Ernie Garateix, Heritage’s CEO, commented, “Several years ago, we implemented a strategy of long-term sustainability which includes a focus on improving profitability, strengthening the balance sheet, and reducing volatility in our financial results. Today, we are generating record earnings, producing substantial capital and are positioned well to continue our momentum into the future. We believe Heritage has proven to be a stronger, better diversified and more resilient business, operating as a super-regional insurance carrier. We have the ability to opportunistically deploy capital across multiple geographies, products and distribution channels, which allows us to allocate capital to the most attractive risk-adjusted return opportunities while maintaining the underwriting discipline that has defined our transformation.”

Mr. Garateix continued, “Our third quarter results demonstrate our earnings resilience as we absorbed higher weather losses and still modestly grew earnings per share. Despite the significant progress that we have made transforming our business and building the sustainable earnings power of the Company, we believe our full value has yet to be realized. As a result, we have repurchased more than one million shares of our common stock year to date. Additionally, our expanding geographic reach and the capabilities we have built position Heritage to capitalize on selective growth opportunities across our markets while remaining selective in areas where competition has intensified.”

•
Generating underwriting profit through rate adequacy and continued selective underwriting.
•
Allocating capital to products and geographies that maximize long-term returns.
•
Targeting a balanced and diversified portfolio.

Ratios

Ceded premium ratio represents ceded premiums as a percentage of gross premiums earned.

Net loss ratio represents net losses and loss adjustment expenses (“LAE”) as a percentage of net premiums earned.

2

Exhibit 99.1

 

Net expense ratio represents policy acquisition costs (“PAC”) and general and administrative (“G&A”) expenses as a percentage of net premiums earned. Ceding commission income is reported as a reduction of PAC and G&A expenses.

Net combined ratio represents the sum of net losses and LAE, PAC and G&A expenses as a percentage of net premiums earned. The net combined ratio is a key measure of underwriting performance traditionally used in the property and casualty industry. A combined ratio under 100% generally reflects profitable underwriting results.

Debt-to-Capital ratio represents total short term and long term interest bearing debt divided by total debt plus stockholders' equity.

Third Quarter 2026 Results:

Third quarter 2026 net income increased to $51.1 million, or $1.72 per diluted share, compared with $50.4 million, or $1.63 per diluted share, in the prior-year quarter. Diluted earnings per share increased 5.5%, benefiting from a 4.0% reduction in diluted shares outstanding primarily from share repurchases. Third quarter results absorbed $15.8 million of pre-tax catastrophe losses, including $9.1 million from Hurricanes Lala and Lowell, and $6.7 million from other catastrophe weather events, primarily the late September Nor’easter, compared with no catastrophe losses in the prior-year quarter. Revenue growth reflected lower ceded premiums, which increased net premiums earned, as well as higher investment income from a larger invested asset base.

Premiums-in-force were $1.40 billion, down 2.8% from $1.44 billion in the third quarter of 2025, primarily due to commercial residential premium reductions resulting from competitive pricing pressure. A reduction in Florida personal lines in-force premiums was more than offset by personal lines growth in other states.

Gross premiums written decreased 3.1% to $322.8 million from $333.2 million in the prior-year quarter, primarily reflecting lower commercial residential premiums, partially offset by growth in personal lines. The Florida commercial residential market remains highly competitive, and management continues to emphasize underwriting discipline and adequate profitability by requiring our commercial residential business to meet Heritage’s pricing and risk standards.

Gross premiums earned were $352.8 million, compared with $362.0 million in the prior-year quarter, reflecting the decline in commercial residential business due to the competitive market conditions discussed above.

Ceded premiums decreased 11.8% to $147.2 million, compared with $166.8 million in the prior-year quarter. The reduction in ceded premiums reflected a full quarter of savings driven by the improved pricing of the Company’s June 2026 catastrophe excess of loss program as well as the decrease in the northeast net quota share program, which was effective December 31, 2025. The catastrophe excess of loss reinsurance placement generated treaty-year expense savings of $63.2 million, of which seven-twelfths will be recognized in calendar year 2026.

Net premiums earned increased 5.4% to $205.6 million from $195.1 million in the prior-year quarter, driven by lower ceded premiums more than offsetting the decrease in gross earned premiums.

Total revenue increased 3.2% to $219.2 million, reflecting higher net premiums earned and increased investment income. Other revenue decreased $2.6 million on lower fee income, and net realized gains decreased $2.8 million, as both were elevated in the prior-year quarter. Net investment income rose 17.3% to $11.4 million in the third quarter of 2026 from $9.7 million in the prior-year quarter, driven by growth in invested assets. Cash and invested assets were $1.4 billion at September 30, 2026, up 8.8% from December 31, 2025. The investment portfolio remains conservatively positioned, emphasizing high-quality fixed-income investments with asset durations closely matched to liabilities.

Net losses and LAE were $81.4 million, up $6.6 million from $74.8 million in the prior-year quarter. The net loss ratio increased 1.3 points to 39.6% from 38.3% in the same quarter last year. Net weather and catastrophe losses for the current accident quarter were $33.6 million, compared with $13.8 million in the prior-year quarter, and included $9.1 million of hurricane losses and $6.7 million of other catastrophe losses. Net favorable prior-year loss development was $14.0 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared with $5.0 million in the prior-year quarter.

3

The net expense ratio improved 1.2 points to 33.4%, from 34.6% in the prior-year quarter. Policy acquisition costs were relatively flat compared to the prior year quarter. General and administrative expenses increased 5.2%, reflecting higher employee compensation and related costs offset by a decrease in corporate insurance expense, primarily from a non-recurring premium refund.

The net combined ratio was relatively flat at 73.0% compared with 72.9% in the prior-year quarter, with a decrease in expense ratio nearly offsetting the increase in loss ratio.

The effective tax rate was 24.5% compared to 26.2% in the prior-year quarter. We calculate the provision for income taxes during interim reporting periods by applying an estimate of the effective tax rate for the full year. The effective tax rate is 1.7 points lower than the prior-year quarter, with the variance driven by permanent items. The effective tax rate can fluctuate throughout the year as income changes and estimates used in each quarterly tax provision are updated with additional information.

About Heritage

Heritage Insurance Holdings, Inc. is a super-regional property and casualty insurance holding company. Through its insurance subsidiaries and a large network of experienced agents, the Company writes approximately $1.4 billion of personal and commercial residential premium across its multi-state footprint covering the northeast, southeast and Hawaii on an admitted basis, with additional excess and surplus lines premium in California, Florida, Hawaii, South Carolina and Texas.
