SAN FRANCISCO, November 9, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) – Structure Therapeutics Inc. (NASDAQ: GPCR), a clinical-stage global biopharmaceutical company developing novel oral small molecule therapeutics for metabolic diseases, with a focus on obesity, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a business update across its oral small molecule obesity portfolio.

 

“With our Phase 3 ACCOMPLISH program underway, we are advancing aleniglipron toward potential registration while building a broader portfolio of differentiated oral small molecule treatments for chronic weight management,” said Raymond Stevens, Ph.D., Chief Executive Officer of Structure Therapeutics. “We are developing our commercial readiness plans and see significant opportunity for aleniglipron in both initial weight loss and long-term weight loss maintenance.”

 

Dr. Stevens continued, “We are also advancing our oral small molecule amylin portfolio by progressing the multiple ascending dose clinical trial of ACCG-2671, preparing to initiate a Phase 1 clinical trial of ACCG-3535 and exploring GLP-1 combination approaches. Our strategy is to develop differentiated monotherapy and combination medicines to address a range of patient needs. We believe convenient oral administration and scalable manufacturing could help expand access to these potential treatments globally.”

 

Recent and Upcoming Milestones

 

Aleniglipron – Oral Small Molecule Selective Glucagon-Like Peptide 1 (GLP-1) Receptor Agonist

 

The Phase 3 ACCOMPLISH program, which initiated in August 2026, comprises two randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled clinical trials designed to evaluate the long-term efficacy and safety of aleniglipron and to support global regulatory submissions for chronic weight management.

 

·ACCOMPLISH-1 is enrolling up to 3,600 adults living with obesity or overweight with at least one weight-related comorbidity. ACCOMPLISH-2 is enrolling up to 1,100 adults living with obesity or overweight and type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM).

 

 

 

 

·In both trials, participants will receive placebo or one of three aleniglipron maintenance doses (45 mg, 90 mg or 180 mg) following a 2.5 mg starting dose and dose escalation at four-week intervals. Topline data are expected in the second half of 2028.

 

In September 2026, the Company reported 72-week results from the ACCESS open-label extension (OLE), a prespecified 36-week extension of the Phase 2b ACCESS clinical trial designed to evaluate the longer-term safety and tolerability of aleniglipron and the durability of weight loss through 72 weeks of treatment. Participants originally randomized to the 45 mg, 90 mg and 120 mg arms in ACCESS who continued into the OLE dosed up to 180 mg. Data demonstrated up to 16.2% mean reduction in body weight in the highest dose group at 72 weeks with no observed plateau in the two top dose groups, and improved tolerability with a 2.5 mg starting dose, including treatment discontinuation rates of less than 5% due to treatment-emergent adverse events.

 

The Company will present two posters on aleniglipron at The Obesity Society’s Annual Meeting, ObesityWeek® 2026, taking place November 14–17, 2026, in National Harbor, Maryland. The presentations will cover the ACCESS OLE and the dose-ranging effects of aleniglipron across the ACCESS trials. Both posters are scheduled for presentation on November 15, 2026.

 

Additional aleniglipron data expected in the fourth quarter of 2026 include topline results from three supplementary clinical trials designed to build on the Phase 2 findings and further characterize aleniglipron’s clinical profile:

 

·Body composition clinical trial: Results from a 44-week study evaluating the effects of aleniglipron on body fat and overall body composition.

 

·T2DM clinical trial: Results from a 30-week study in adults living with T2DM and obesity or overweight.

 

·SWITCH clinical trial: Results evaluating the transition from an approved injectable GLP-1 receptor agonist to once-daily oral aleniglipron for weight loss maintenance. This study assesses different aleniglipron starting doses and weight loss maintenance over 12 weeks.

 

Oral Small Molecule Dual Amylin and Calcitonin Receptor Agonists

 

In September 2026, the Company reported topline results from the single ascending dose (SAD) portion of its Phase 1/2a clinical trial of ACCG-2671, an oral small molecule dual amylin and calcitonin receptor agonist (DACRA). The SAD portion evaluated the safety, tolerability, pharmacokinetics (PK) and exploratory pharmacodynamics of single doses of 1, 2, 5 or 10 mg of ACCG-2671 or placebo in 31 healthy adult participants without obesity. ACCG-2671 demonstrated a half-life of approximately 6 days. No serious adverse events were reported.

 

The Company has begun dosing in the 12-week multiple ascending dose (MAD) portion of the Phase 1/2a trial, a randomized, placebo-controlled study evaluating the safety, tolerability and PK of multiple ascending oral doses of ACCG-2671 administered for 84 days across multiple cohorts of participants living with obesity. The trial will evaluate daily and weekly dosing regimens and different titration approaches. It also includes a cohort evaluating ACCG-2671 added to a stable dose of an injectable GLP-1 receptor agonist. Topline data from the MAD portion are expected in the first half of 2027.

 

The Company plans to initiate a Phase 1 clinical trial of its second oral small molecule DACRA candidate, ACCG-3535, in the fourth quarter of 2026.

 

 

 

 

In addition, the Company expects to initiate a clinical trial evaluating a combination of an oral small molecule amylin and GLP-1 receptor agonists in 2027.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights

 

Cash Position: Cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments totaled $1.2 billion as of September 30, 2026, compared to $1.3 billion as of June 30, 2026. The Company expects its current cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments to fund projected operations and key clinical milestones through the end of 2028, including costs related to the Phase 3 registrational program for aleniglipron in chronic weight management, but excludes additional costs related to pre-commercialization activities including commercial manufacturing.

 

Research and Development (R&D) Expenses: R&D expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $134.6 million, as compared to $59.0 million for the same period in 2025. The increase in R&D expenses was primarily due to increases related to clinical trial costs, preclinical research and development expenses, and employee expenses.

 

General and Administrative (G&A) Expenses: G&A expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $21.8 million, as compared to $14.8 million for the same period in 2025. The increase in G&A expenses was primarily due to increases in employee expenses as the Company expanded its infrastructure to drive and support the growth in operations.

 

Net Loss: Net loss for the third quarter of 2026 totaled $144.8 million, with non-cash share-based compensation expense of $12.7 million, compared to a net loss of $65.7 million for the same period in 2025, with non-cash share-based compensation expense of $7.5 million.

 

About Structure Therapeutics

 

Structure Therapeutics is a science-driven clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on discovering and developing innovative oral small molecule treatments for chronic metabolic conditions with significant unmet medical needs. Utilizing its next-generation structure-based drug discovery platform, the Company has established a robust GPCR-targeted pipeline, featuring multiple wholly-owned proprietary clinical-stage oral small molecule compounds designed to surpass the scalability limitations of traditional biologic and peptide therapies and be accessible to more people living with obesity around the world.
