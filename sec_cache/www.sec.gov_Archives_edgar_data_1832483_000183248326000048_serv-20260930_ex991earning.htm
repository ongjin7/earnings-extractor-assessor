Serve Robotics Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results

•Delivered over 300% revenue growth in Q3 year over year and raised full year revenue guidance for 2026 to $12 million.

•Tripled daily average deliveries in Q3 with new delivery partners, and exclusively launched first international city in Australia in October.

•Improved gross margin sequentially, as the mix of higher-margin recurring revenue jumped to nearly 70% of all revenues.

•Ended Q3 with over $198 million in cash and marketable securities, reflecting strong balance sheet and operational flexibility.


SAN FRANCISCO, November 5, 2026 -- Serve Robotics Inc. (the “Company” or “Serve”) (Nasdaq: SERV), a leading autonomy and robotics company, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

“Serve is getting stronger on every front: demand for our robots is growing, the fleet costs less to operate, and we are opening new ways to generate revenue from what we have built,” said Dr. Ali Kashani, Serve’s Co-founder and CEO. “We are raising our full-year revenue guidance to $12 million. And, as we announced today, with the launch of Serve Physical AI Services, the technology behind our fleets is now available to other robot makers, a significant new opportunity for us.”

“This quarter was about execution,” said Brian Read, Chief Financial Officer of Serve. “Q3 revenue exceeded our expectations, and we drove operating expenses down while delivering more than 100 percentage points of sequential improvement in fleet margin. We believe the gains we made this quarter are structural, and they form the foundation for a long-term trend of improving unit economics.”

Business and Financial Highlights
•Revenue Growth: Reported revenue of $2.8 million, an increase of 313% year-over-year.
•Revenue Mix: Recurring revenue accounted for 69% of total revenue in Q3 and continued to outpace non-recurring revenue, while branding revenue grew nearly 80% sequentially.
•Healthcare Progress: Generated strong Q3 revenue in consistent with our expectations, entered into 12 multi-year contract extensions with hospital customers, and added 4 new hospitals year-to-date through Q3, demonstrating continued demand for the Company's healthcare automation platform.
•Delivery Volume: Among new delivery platform partners, daily delivery volume tripled and the number of merchants grew 2.5x in Q3.
•Balance Sheet: Maintained a strong liquidity position of $198.0 million as of September 30, 2026.
•Outstanding Shares: Approximately 88 million shares of common stock outstanding as of September 30, 2026.

Outlook

•Full year 2026 revenue guidance increased to $12 million, from the previously provided range of $9 million to $10 million, reflecting strong robot demand and growth across a diversified portfolio of revenue streams.

•Full year 2026 non-GAAP operating expense guidance lowered to $150 million, compared with prior guidance of $160 to $170 million provided at the beginning of the year, reflecting strong cost discipline.

About Serve
Serve (Nasdaq: SERV) designs and operates autonomous robots that navigate complex, human-centric environments. Since spinning off from Uber in 2021, Serve has deployed more than 2,000 robots across the U.S., reaching a population of approximately 3 million and supporting delivery for more than 4,000 restaurants. In 2026, Serve acquired Diligent Robotics, Inc., expanding its operations beyond sidewalk delivery into indoor service robots used in hospitals. Serve designs both the hardware and software behind its robots, enabling them to work safely in public and private environments at scale.

For further information about Serve (Nasdaq: SERV), please visit www.serverobotics.com or follow us on social media via X (Twitter), Instagram, or LinkedIn @serverobotics.
