VICI PROPERTIES INC. ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS
- Announced Agreement to Add Highfield Investment Group to VICI's Tenant Roster -
- Completed $1.75 Billion Investment Grade Senior Notes Offering -
- Increased Annual Dividend for 9th Consecutive Year -
- Raised Guidance for Full Year 2026 -

NEW YORK, NY – October 28, 2026 – VICI Properties Inc. (NYSE: VICI) (“VICI Properties”, “VICI” or the “Company”), an experiential real estate investment trust, today reported results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. All per share amounts included herein are on a per diluted common share basis unless otherwise stated.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial and Operating Highlights
•Total revenues increased 5.8% year-over-year to $1.1 billion
•Net income attributable to common stockholders decreased 55.1% year-over-year to $342.5 million and, on a per share basis, decreased 56.4% year-over-year to $0.31 due to the impact of the change in the CECL allowance for the quarter ended September 30, 2026
•AFFO attributable to common stockholders increased 7.5% year-over-year to $685.2 million and, on a per share basis, increased 4.3% year-over-year to $0.62
•Declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.46 per share, representing a 2.2% year-over-year increase
•Issued $1.75 billion of investment grade senior notes to refinance 2026 maturities
•Announced an agreement to enter into a new triple-net master lease with Highfield Investment Group, in connection with Century Casinos' agreement to sell the operations of Century Mile Racetrack and Century Downs Racetrack, located in Alberta, Canada, to Highfield, adding to VICI's tenant roster upon closing
•Ended the quarter with $270.3 million in cash and cash equivalents
•Raised AFFO guidance for full year 2026 to between $2,695 million and $2,705 million, or between $2.47 and $2.48 per diluted share
•Subsequent to quarter-end, our Board of Directors authorized a share repurchase program for up to $2.0 billion in shares
CEO Comments
Edward Pitoniak, Chief Executive Officer of VICI Properties, said, “In the third quarter of 2026, we grew our quarterly revenue by 5.8% and our AFFO per share by 4.3% year-over-year, once again demonstrating the flow-through efficiency of our business model, and we were pleased to announce our 9th consecutive annual dividend increase since our IPO almost nine years ago. Our commitment to situational readiness positioned us to refinance all of our remaining 2026 maturities through a $1.75 billion investment grade notes offering in August at an adjusted weighted average interest rate of 5.42%. In September, we announced our agreement to enter into a new lease with Highfield Investment Group, and we look forward to welcoming Highfield to our tenant roster upon closing, further diversifying our tenant base and further strengthening our presence in the dynamic region of Alberta. At VICI, we are always working to strengthen our portfolio while keeping our balance sheet ready to grow alongside new and existing partners. We believe that discipline will enable us to keep compounding value for our stockholders for years to come.”


Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Total Revenues
Total revenues were $1.1 billion for the quarter, an increase of 5.8% compared to $1.0 billion for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. Total revenues for the quarter included $132.0 million of non-cash leasing and financing adjustments and $19.0 million of other income.
Net Income Attributable to Common Stockholders
Net income attributable to common stockholders was $342.5 million for the quarter, or $0.31 per share, compared to $762.0 million, or $0.71 per share, for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. The year-over-year decrease in net income was driven, on an absolute basis, by the $478.2 million aggregate change in the CECL allowance for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 compared to the quarter ended September 30, 2025.
Funds from Operations (“FFO”)
FFO attributable to common stockholders was $342.5 million for the quarter, or $0.31 per share, compared to $762.0 million, or $0.71 per share, for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. The year-over-year decrease in FFO was driven, on an absolute basis, by the $478.2 million aggregate change in the CECL allowance for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 compared to the quarter ended September 30, 2025.
Adjusted Funds from Operations (“AFFO”)
AFFO attributable to common stockholders was $685.2 million for the quarter, an increase of 7.5% compared to $637.6 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. AFFO per share was $0.62 for the quarter, an increase of 4.3% compared to $0.60 for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.
Third Quarter 2026 Investment and Portfolio Activity
Investment and Portfolio Activity
On September 28, 2026, VICI announced, in connection with Century Casinos, Inc.’s (NASDAQ: CNTY) (“Century Casinos”) agreement to sell the operations of Century Mile Racetrack and Century Downs Racetrack (together, the “Century Canada Racinos”), located in Alberta, Canada, to a subsidiary of Highfield Investment Group, Inc. (“Highfield”), its agreement to enter into a new separate triple-net master lease with a subsidiary of Highfield with respect to the real property of the Century Canada Racinos (the “Highfield Master Lease”) and to amend the Master Lease between VICI and Century Casinos (the “Century Master Lease”).
The Highfield Master Lease will have an initial annual base rent of C$10.7 million (approximately US$7.5 million as of the day prior to the announcement) and, if closing occurs after January 1, 2027, such base rent shall escalate as provided for in the Century Master Lease on such date. Upon closing, the Highfield Master Lease will begin a new 20-year initial lease term with four 5-year tenant renewal options. The other economic terms of the Highfield Master Lease will be substantially similar to the terms for the Century Canada Racinos under the Century Master Lease, including escalation equal to the greater of 1.25% and the change in Canadian CPI (capped at 2.50%) and a minimum capital expenditure requirement equal to 1.0% of annual net revenue at each property. The Highfield Master Lease will be guaranteed by Highfield Investment Group, Inc.
2

Upon closing, the Century Master Lease will be amended to account for Century Casinos' divestiture of the operations of the Century Canada Racinos, which will result in a corresponding reduction of the annual base rent under the Century Master Lease by C$10.7 million (approximately US$7.5 million as of the day prior to the announcement) with adjustments to reflect the escalated rent if the transaction closing occurs after January 1, 2027. There will be no change to the aggregate rent collected by VICI. The transaction is subject to customary closing conditions and regulatory approvals and is expected to be completed in the fourth quarter of 2026 or the first quarter of 2027.
Third Quarter 2026 and Subsequent Capital Markets Activity
On August 5, 2026, VICI priced the offering of $1.75 billion aggregate principal amount of senior unsecured notes (the “August 2026 Notes”). On August 14, 2026, VICI issued the August 2026 Notes comprised of (i) $900.0 million aggregate principal amount of 5.400% Senior Notes due 2031, which mature on October 15, 2031, and (ii) $850.0 million aggregate principal amount of 5.750% Senior Notes due 2036, which mature on October 15, 2036. The weighted average interest rate for the August 2026 Notes is 5.570%, and the adjusted weighted average interest rate, after taking into account the impact of forward-starting interest rate swaps and treasury locks, is 5.422%. The net proceeds of the August 2026 Notes were used to redeem the then-outstanding (i) $480.5 million aggregate principal amount of 4.500% Exchange Notes due 2026, (ii) $1,250.0 million aggregate principal amount of 4.250% November 2019 Notes due 2026, and (iii) $19.5 million aggregate principal amount of 4.500% MGP OP Notes due 2026.
During the nine months ended September 30, 2026, VICI entered into a combination of forward-starting interest rate swap agreements and treasury locks with an aggregate notional amount of $1.1 billion. In connection with the August 2026 Notes offering, VICI settled the outstanding forward-starting interest rate swaps and treasury locks for total proceeds of $20.0 million. Since the forward-starting swaps and treasury locks were hedging the interest rate risk on the anticipated issuance of the August 2026 Notes, the unrealized gain, which was recorded in Accumulated other comprehensive income on VICI's consolidated balance sheets, will be amortized over the term of the respective derivative instruments as a reduction in interest expense.
Subsequent to quarter-end, on October 27, 2026, VICI’s Board of Directors authorized a share repurchase program for up to $2.0 billion in shares of the Company’s common stock, which will expire on September 30, 2029. Repurchases under the share repurchase program may be made from time to time at management’s discretion through open market purchases, privately negotiated transactions, Rule 10b5-1 plans or other methods, in each case in accordance with applicable securities laws. The share repurchase program does not obligate VICI to repurchase any particular amount of common stock and may be suspended or discontinued at any time.

2026 Guidance
The Company is raising its AFFO guidance for the full year 2026. In determining AFFO, the Company adjusts for certain items that are otherwise included in determining net income attributable to common stockholders, the most comparable generally accepted accounting principles in the United States (“GAAP”) financial measure. In reliance on the exception provided by applicable rules, the Company does not provide guidance for GAAP net income, the most comparable GAAP financial measure, or a reconciliation of 2026 AFFO to GAAP net income because we are unable to predict with reasonable certainty the amount of the change in non-cash allowance for credit losses under ASU No. 2016-13 - Financial Instruments—Credit Losses (Topic 326) (“ASC 326”) for a future period. The non-cash change in allowance for credit losses under ASC 326 with respect to a future period is dependent upon future events that are entirely outside of the Company’s control and may not be reliably predicted, including its tenants’ respective financial performance, fluctuations in the trading price of their common stock, credit ratings and outlook (each to the extent applicable), as well as broader macroeconomic performance. Based on past results and, as disclosed in our historical financial results, the impact of these adjustments could be material, individually or in the aggregate, to the Company’s reported GAAP results. For more information, see “Non-GAAP Financial Measures.”
The Company estimates AFFO for the year ending December 31, 2026 will be between $2,695 million and $2,705 million, or between $2.47 and $2.48 per diluted common share. Guidance does not include the impact on operating results from any pending acquisitions without announced expected closing dates, possible future acquisitions or dispositions, capital markets activity, or other non-recurring transactions.

About VICI Properties
VICI Properties Inc. is an S&P 500® experiential real estate investment trust that owns one of the largest portfolios of market-leading gaming, hospitality, wellness, entertainment and leisure destinations, including Caesars Palace Las Vegas, MGM Grand and the Venetian Resort Las Vegas, three of the most iconic entertainment facilities on the Las Vegas Strip. VICI Properties owns 103 experiential assets across a geographically diverse portfolio consisting of 63 gaming properties and 40 other experiential properties across the United States and Canada. The portfolio is comprised of approximately 130 million square feet and features approximately 66,000 hotel rooms and over 700 restaurants, bars, nightclubs and sportsbooks. Its properties are occupied by industry-leading gaming, leisure and hospitality operators under long-term, triple-net lease agreements. VICI Properties has a growing array of real estate and financing partnerships with leading operators in other experiential sectors, including Cabot, Cain, Canyon Ranch, Chelsea Piers, Club Med, Great Wolf Resorts, Homefield, Kalahari Resorts, and Lucky Strike Entertainment. VICI Properties also owns four championship golf courses and approximately 33 acres of undeveloped and underdeveloped land adjacent to the Las Vegas Strip. VICI Properties’ goal is to create the highest quality and most productive experiential real estate portfolio through a strategy of partnering with the highest quality experiential place makers and operators. For additional information, please visit www.viciproperties.com.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
This press release presents Funds From Operations (“FFO”), FFO per share, Adjusted Funds From Operations (“AFFO”), AFFO per share and Adjusted EBITDA, which are not required by, or presented in accordance with, generally accepted accounting principles in the United States (“GAAP”). These are non-GAAP financial measures and should not be construed as alternatives to net income or as an indicator of operating performance (as determined in accordance with GAAP). We believe FFO, FFO per share, AFFO, AFFO per share and Adjusted EBITDA provide a meaningful perspective of the underlying operating performance of our business.
FFO is a non-GAAP financial measure that is considered a supplemental measure for the real estate industry and a supplement to GAAP measures. Consistent with the definition used by the National Association of Real Estate Investment Trusts (Nareit), we define FFO as our net income (or loss) attributable to common stockholders (computed in accordance with GAAP) excluding (i) gains (or losses) from sales of certain real estate assets, (ii) depreciation and amortization related to real estate, (iii) gains and losses from change in control and (iv) impairment write-downs of certain real estate assets and investments in entities when the impairment is directly attributable to decreases in the value of depreciable real estate held by the entity.
AFFO is a non-GAAP financial measure that we use as a supplemental operating measure to evaluate our performance. We calculate AFFO by adding or subtracting from FFO non-cash leasing and financing adjustments, non-cash change in allowance for credit losses, non-cash stock-based compensation expense, transaction costs incurred in connection with the acquisition of real estate investments, amortization of debt issuance costs and original issue discount, other non-cash interest expense, capitalized interest on real estate under development, non-real estate depreciation (which is comprised of the depreciation related to our golf course operations), capital expenditures (which are comprised of additions to property, plant and equipment related to our golf course operations), impairment charges related to non-depreciable real estate, gains (or losses) on debt extinguishment and
8

interest rate swap settlements, other gains (or losses), deferred income tax provision (or benefit), other non-recurring non-cash transactions and non-cash adjustments attributable to non-controlling interest with respect to certain of the foregoing.
We calculate Adjusted EBITDA by adding or subtracting from AFFO contractual interest expense (including the impact of the forward-starting interest rate swaps and treasury locks) and interest income (collectively, interest expense, net), current income tax expense and adjustments attributable to non-controlling interests.
These non-GAAP financial measures: (i) do not represent cash flow from operations as defined by GAAP; (ii) should not be considered as an alternative to net income as a measure of operating performance or to cash flows from operating, investing and financing activities; and (iii) are not alternatives to cash flow as a measure of liquidity. In addition, these measures should not be viewed as measures of liquidity, nor do they measure our ability to fund all of our cash needs, including our ability to make cash distributions to our stockholders, to fund capital improvements, or to make interest payments on our indebtedness. Investors are also cautioned that FFO, FFO per share, AFFO, AFFO per share and Adjusted EBITDA, as presented, may not be comparable to similarly titled measures reported by other real estate companies, including REITs, due to the fact that not all real estate companies use the same definitions. Our presentation of these measures does not replace the presentation of our financial results in accordance with GAAP.
