EXHIBIT 99.1

Exponent Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

MENLO PARK, Calif., Oct. 29, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Exponent, Inc. (Nasdaq: EXPO) today reported financial results for the third quarter of fiscal year 2026 ended October 2, 2026.

“Exponent delivered another strong quarter, with double-digit growth in revenues and earnings, reflecting continued demand for our specialized, integrated engineering and scientific expertise,” stated Dr. Catherine Corrigan, Chief Executive Officer. “User research studies continued to be a strong contributor to our growth, as clients move quickly to understand how people interact with technology across a widening range of devices and applications. Reactive work also grew, on top of a strong prior-year quarter, with increased dispute-related and failure analysis demand in the chemical, consumer products and transportation industries.

“These results reflect the durable, long-term trends shaping our business, including rapid technological innovation, increasing complexity, investment in critical infrastructure, and rising societal expectations for safety and reliability. As artificial intelligence and other digital technologies become increasingly embedded into physical systems, the digital and physical worlds are converging. Exponent is differentiated at that convergence, where data and computation meet real-world evidence, and where technology must ultimately work for the user. Our multidisciplinary scientists and engineers bring together deep domain expertise, scientific rigor, and an understanding of complex physical systems to help clients innovate with confidence, solve their most challenging problems, and manage high-consequence risk,” Dr. Corrigan continued.

Third Quarter Financial Results

Total revenues and revenues before reimbursements for the third quarter of 2026 increased 18% to $173.7 million and 12% to $153.1 million, respectively, as compared to $147.1 million and $137.1 million in the third quarter of 2025.

Net income increased to $30.9 million, or $0.64 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2026, as compared to $28.0 million, or $0.55 per diluted share, in the same period of 2025. The tax benefit associated with share-based awards was immaterial in both the third quarter of 2026 and 2025. Inclusive of the tax benefit, Exponent’s consolidated tax rate was 28.0% in the third quarter of 2026, as compared to 27.4% for the same period in 2025.

EBITDA1 increased to $45.0 million, or 29.4% of revenues before reimbursements, in the third quarter of 2026, as compared to $38.8 million, or 28.3% of revenues before reimbursements in the third quarter of 2025.

Year to Date Financial Results

Total revenues and revenues before reimbursements for the first three quarters of 2026 increased 18% to $511.6 million and 11% to $453.7 million, respectively, as compared to $434.6 million and $407.4 million in the same period of 2025.

Net income increased to $89.9 million, or $1.83 per diluted share, through the first three quarters of 2026, as compared to $81.2 million, or $1.58 per diluted share, in the same period of 2025. During the first three quarters of 2026, we realized a negative tax impact associated with share-based awards of $0.8 million, as compared to $0.4 million during the same period one year ago. Inclusive of the tax impact associated with share-based awards, Exponent’s consolidated tax rate was 28.7% through the first three quarters of 2026, as compared to 28.2% for the same period last year.

EBITDA1 increased to $130.8 million, or 28.8% of revenues before reimbursements, through the first three quarters of 2026, as compared to $113.4 million, or 27.8% of revenues before reimbursements, in the same period of 2025.

Through the first three quarters of 2026, Exponent paid $46.1 million in dividends, repurchased $146.1 million of common stock, and closed the period with $114.7 million in cash and cash equivalents.  

In a separate press release today, Exponent announced its quarterly cash dividend of $0.31 to be paid on December 18, 2026, and reiterated its intent to continue to pay quarterly dividends.

Business Overview

Exponent’s engineering and other scientific segment represented 85% of the Company’s revenues before reimbursements in the third quarter and through the first three quarters of 2026. Revenues before reimbursements in this segment increased 13% in the third quarter and through the first three quarters of 2026, as compared to the prior year period. Growth during the quarter was driven by strong user research activity spanning a broad set of challenges, including health applications, dynamic and unstructured real-world environments, and sensory perception. Dispute-related services involving chemical facilities, consumer products and advanced transportation technologies also drove growth in the quarter, as clients rely on Exponent in critical, high-stakes situations.

Exponent’s environmental and health segment represented 15% of the Company’s revenues before reimbursements in the third quarter and through the first three quarters of 2026. Revenues before reimbursements in this segment increased 3% in the third quarter and 4% through the first three quarters of 2026, as compared to the prior year period. Growth in this segment was driven by increased projects assessing the effects of chemicals on human health and the environment.

Business Outlook

“Our ability to scale our operating model in response to market demand resulted in 77% utilization and expanded profitability during the third quarter of 2026. We are encouraged by the market opportunities across the industries we serve and continue to strategically build upon our diversified portfolio of talent and capabilities,” commented Eric Anderson, Chief Financial Officer. “As we close out 2026, we are raising our full-year revenue and margin guidance, reflecting the strength of our year-to-date performance and our outlook for the remainder of the year. Looking ahead, we remain confident in Exponent’s long-term growth trajectory and our ability to capitalize on the strong demand for our differentiated expertise.”

For the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 as compared to the same period one year prior, Exponent anticipates:

  • Revenues before reimbursements to grow 8% to 10%; and,
  • EBITDA1 to be 26.3% to 26.8% of revenues before reimbursements.

For the full fiscal year 2026 as compared to fiscal year 2025, Exponent anticipates:

  • Revenues before reimbursements to grow 10% to 11%; and,
  • EBITDA1 to be 28.0% to 28.3% of revenues before reimbursements.

“Rapid innovation continues to reshape industries as companies race to make physical products more intelligent, while expectations for safety, reliability, and performance continue to rise. Exponent is built for this moment. We help clients navigate these increasingly complex challenges, whether validating AI-enabled systems, strengthening critical infrastructure, or understanding how people interact with the technologies around them. Across these areas, our integrated platform brings the scientific and engineering expertise needed to deliver explainability, clarity, and trust. This distinctive ecosystem, together with strong long-term growth drivers, positions Exponent to deliver sustainable value for our clients and shareholders,” concluded Dr. Corrigan.

Use of non-GAAP Financial Measures 1

EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measure defined by the Company as net income before income taxes, interest income, depreciation, and amortization. EBITDAS is a non-GAAP financial measure defined by the Company as EBITDA before stock-based compensation. The Company regards EBITDA and EBITDAS as useful measures of operating performance and cash flow to complement operating income, net income, and other GAAP financial performance measures. Additionally, management believes that EBITDA and EBITDAS provide meaningful comparisons of past, present, and future operating results. Generally, a non-GAAP financial measure is a numerical measure of a company's performance, financial position, or cash flow that either excludes or includes amounts that are not normally excluded or included in the most directly comparable measure calculated and presented in accordance with GAAP. These measures, however, should be considered in addition to, and not as a substitute or superior to, operating income, cash flows, or other measures of financial performance prepared in accordance with GAAP.

Exponent has provided its outlook regarding EBITDA as a percentage of revenues before reimbursements. The Company has not reconciled this non-GAAP financial measure to the corresponding GAAP financial measure because guidance for the various reconciling items is not provided and the Company is unable to estimate with reasonable certainty the effect of these items without unreasonable effort. For example, the Company is unable to estimate with reasonable certainty the impact of equity awards on Exponent’s taxes without unreasonable effort. These items are uncertain, depend on various factors, and may have a material effect on Exponent’s results computed in accordance with GAAP. A reconciliation between the historical GAAP and non-GAAP financial measures presented in this release is provided in the financial tables at the end of this release.
