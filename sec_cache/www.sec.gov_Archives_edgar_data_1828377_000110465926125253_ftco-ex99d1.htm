FORTITUDE GOLD REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS

COLORADO SPRINGS – November 10, 2026 - Fortitude Gold Corporation (OTCQB: FTCO) (the “Company”) today reported its third quarter 2026 results including $10.3 million net sales, $3.9 million in exploration expense, $0.9 million cash dividends to shareholders, $5.2 million mine gross profit, and a cash balance at September 30, 2026, of $8.5 million. The Company recorded net income of $2.0 million, or $0.07 per share during the quarter and confirmed its previously announced preliminary 2026 third quarter production of 2,139 gold ounces. Fortitude Gold is a gold producer, developer, and explorer with operations in Nevada, U.S.A. offering investors exposure to both gold production and substantial dividend yield.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Highlights

•2,139 gold ounces produced
•Net income of $2.0 million, or $0.07 per share
•$10.3 million net sales
•$8.5 million cash balance at September 30, 2026
•$27.3 million working capital at September 30, 2026
•$5.2 million mine gross profit
•$3.9 million exploration expenditures
•$1,682 total cash cost after by-product credits per gold ounce sold for the Isabella Pearl Mine
•$2,882 per ounce total all-in sustaining cost for the Isabella Pearl Mine
•$1,930 total cash cost after by-product credits per gold ounce sold for the County Line Mine
•$1,985 per ounce total all-in sustaining cost for the County Line Mine
•$0.9 million dividends paid
•611 ounces of gold rounds/bullion held at September 30, 2026

* The calculation of our cash cost and all-in sustaining cost per ounce contained in this press release is a non-GAAP financial measure. Please see "Management's Discussion and Analysis and Results of Operations" contained in the Company’s recently filed Form 10-K for a complete discussion and reconciliation of the non-GAAP measures.

During the third quarter, Fortitude Gold produced a total of 2,139 ounces of gold.  At the Isabella Pearl Mine, Fortitude Gold sold 1,221 gold ounces at a total cash cost of $1,682 per ounce (after by-product credits) and an all-in-sustaining-cost per ounce of $2,882.  Sales from the County Line Mine included 1,216 gold ounces at a total cash cost of $1,930 per ounce (after by-product credits) and an all-in-sustaining-cost per ounce of $1,985.  Realized average gold metal prices during the quarter for the Isabella Pearl Mine were $4,285, while the County Line Mine averaged $4,258 per ounce gold (1).  

Mine operations at Isabella Pearl during the quarter were directed at both Scarlet South and the Isabella Pearl Deep mineralization.  County Line operations focused on both the main pit layback as well as the East pit.  Mineralization from these mining areas is being delivered and placed on the Isabella Pearl heap leach pad for processing.  The Company is nearing the bottom of the Pearl Deep mineralization and expects to conclude operations in this area in the coming months.  Scarlet South operations are estimated to continue into the first half of 2027.  The County Line main pit waste rock layback project is estimated to conclude in 2027, depending on final mine sequencing of Pearl Deep and Scarlet South.  During this layback project, the County Line East pit is targeted to continue delivering mineralization to the Isabella Pearl heap leach pad for processing.

Federal and state permitting efforts continued at the Company’s Golden Mile property, where a new mine is targeted for construction in 2027, dependent on permit timing.  The Company is also permitting an increase in exploration acreage at both the East Camp Douglas and Isabella Pearl properties.

“We had a very busy and productive third quarter including the ongoing expansion of our heap leach pad,” said Fortitude Gold CEO and President, Mr. Jason Reid.  “Progress of our various permitting efforts is encouraging. Our Golden Mile Permit, currently with the Bureau of Land Management, should begin the six-month National Environmental Policy Act (“NEPA”) review period soon.  We are excited about the prospect of building our Golden Mile mine and we are encouraged by the progress toward obtaining the remaining permits necessary to advance the project.  In addition, we are optimistic by the progress toward securing the East Camp Douglas exploration EA permit from the Bureau of Land Management. The permit would significantly expand our exploration acreage at East Camp Douglas and provide additional opportunities to advance the property's exploration efforts.  We are positioned to potentially be building a new mine at Golden Mile and launching our Company’s largest drill campaign at East Camp Douglas in 2027.”

About Fortitude Gold Corp.:

Fortitude Gold is a U.S. based gold producer targeting projects with low operating costs, high margins, and strong returns on capital. The Company’s strategy is to grow organically, remain debt-free, and distribute dividends. The Company’s Nevada Mining Unit consists of nine high-grade gold properties.  Fortitude Gold owns 100% of its properties, except for East Camp Douglas, which is held in a joint venture with Fortitude owning 60%.  The Isabella Pearl Project, which includes the Isabella Pearl and Scarlet South mines, and County Line Project, which includes the County Line and East Pit Mines, are currently in production in Mineral and Nye Counties, Nevada.  Nevada, U.S.A. is among the world’s premier mining friendly jurisdictions.
