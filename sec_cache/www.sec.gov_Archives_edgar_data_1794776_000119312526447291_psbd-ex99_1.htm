Palmer Square Capital BDC Inc. Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Declares Fourth Quarter 2026 Base Dividend of $0.36 Per Share with Supplemental Dividend

Expected to be Announced in December

MISSION WOODS, Kansas, November 4, 2026 — Palmer Square Capital BDC Inc. (NYSE: PSBD) (“PSBD” or the “Company”), an externally managed business development company, today announced its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Financial and Operating Highlights

•
Total investment income of $26.6 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $31.7 million for the prior year period
•
Net investment income of $11.6 million or $0.38 per share for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $13.6 million or $0.43 per share for the prior year period
•
Net asset value of $13.34 per share as of September 30, 2026, compared to $13.21 per share as of June 30, 2026
•
Total net realized and unrealized gains of $2.4 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to losses of $10.3 million for the third quarter of 2025
•
As of September 30, 2026, total assets were $1.1 billion and total net assets were $403.4 million
•
Debt-to-equity as of September 30, 2026 was 1.63x, compared to 1.71x as of June 30, 2026
•
Paid cash distributions to stockholders totaling $0.38 per share for the third quarter of 2026
•
Declared a fourth quarter regular base dividend distribution of $0.36 per share, payable on January 13, 2027, to stockholders of record as of December 28, 2026. In accordance with our dividend policy, we expect to announce a supplemental dividend in December
•
In the third quarter, we had only $578.7 thousand of recurring payment-in-kind interest income, or 2.18% of total investment income

 

“Palmer Square Capital BDC delivered strong financial performance in the third quarter, supported by our active portfolio management and capital allocation strategy,” said Christopher D. Long, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of PSBD. “During the quarter, we repurchased approximately $5.2 million of shares, demonstrating our commitment to enhancing shareholder value against a fluid market backdrop. Our differentiated strategy was built to navigate environments like this, where portfolio flexibility and liquidity are key to driving performance. With a vantage point across the leveraged loan and private credit markets, we are well-positioned to selectively deploy capital and continue delivering value for shareholders as market conditions evolve.”

Portfolio and Investment Activity

As of September 30, 2026, we had 278 investments in 200 portfolio companies with an aggregate fair value of approximately $1.1 billion. Based on a total fair value of $1.1 billion, including short-term investments, the portfolio consisted of approximately 86.8% first lien senior secured debt investments, 5.2% second lien senior secured debt investments, 5.6% short-term investments, 0.9% collateralized loan obligation structured credit funds (“CLOs”) mezzanine and equity investments, 0.6% corporate bond investments, and 0.9% equity investments.

As of September 30, 2026, 98% of the long-term investments based on fair value in our portfolio were at floating rates. At the end of the third quarter, there was one portfolio company on non-accrual status. As of September 30, 2026, the weighted average total yield to maturity of debt and income producing securities at fair value was 11.99%, and weighted average total yield to maturity of debt and income producing securities at amortized cost was 8.38%. For the third quarter of 2026, the principal amount of new investments funded was $115.0 million which included 31 investments at an average value of approximately $3.5 million. For this period, the Company had $165.8 million aggregate principal amount in sales and repayments.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $1.9 million in cash and cash equivalents and approximately $656.9 million in total aggregate principal amount of debt outstanding. Subject to borrowing base and other restrictions, the Company had available liquidity, consisting of cash and undrawn capacity on credit facilities of approximately $195.0 million compared to $19.4 million of unfunded investment commitments as of September 30, 2026.

Recent Developments

On November 4, 2026, PSBD’s Board of Directors announced that it had declared a fourth quarter regular base dividend distribution of $0.36 per share, payable on January 13, 2027, to stockholders of record as of December 28, 2026. We expect to announce an additional quarterly supplemental dividend distribution for the fourth quarter of 2026 in December.

About Palmer Square Capital BDC Inc.

Palmer Square Capital BDC Inc. (NYSE: PSBD) is an externally managed, non-diversified closed-end management investment company that primarily lends to and invests in corporate debt loans, including but not limited to large private U.S. companies in the broadly syndicated loan market, as well as the direct large cap private credit market. PSBD has elected to be regulated as a business development company under the Investment Company Act of 1940. PSBD’s investment objective is to maximize total return, comprised of current income and capital appreciation. PSBD’s current investment focus is guided by two strategies that facilitate its investment opportunities and core competencies: (1) investing in corporate debt loans and, to a lesser extent, (2) investing in other debt securities which may include collateralized loan obligation debt and equity. PSBD’s investment activities are managed by its investment adviser, Palmer Square BDC Advisor LLC, an affiliate of Palmer Square Capital Management LLC.
