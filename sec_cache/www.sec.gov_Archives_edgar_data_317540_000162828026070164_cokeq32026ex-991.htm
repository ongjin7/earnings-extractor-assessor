Third Quarter and First Nine Months 2026 Review
CHARLOTTE, November 4, 2026 – Coca‑Cola Consolidated, Inc. (NASDAQ: COKE) today reported operating results for the third quarter ended October 2, 2026 and the first nine months of fiscal 2026.

"Our third quarter results reflect the strength of our business and the dedication of our teammates to serving our customers and consumers every day," said J. Frank Harrison, III, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer. "Our net sales growth reflects a healthy balance of volume and price which, combined with our focus on local execution, continues to position us for long-term growth. We remain committed to investing for the long term, including the addition of our new glass bottle production line in Indianapolis, while continuing to strengthen our balance sheet. This year, through October, we have reduced our debt by $450 million."

Volume increased 1.4% in the third quarter of 2026 and 7.1% in the first nine months of 2026. On an adjusted(b) basis, volume increased 5.1% in the first nine months of 2026. Our Sparkling category volume increased 1.5% in the third quarter of 2026 and 6.7% in the first nine months of 2026, or 4.6% on an adjusted(b) basis. Sparkling volume growth was led by our zero-sugar and flavor offerings. Still category volume increased 1.2% in the third quarter of 2026 and 8.5% in the first nine months of 2026, or 6.6% on an adjusted(b) basis. The Still category volume growth was driven by strong performance primarily from our Energy and Protein products. In addition, we estimate the shift in timing of the Fourth of July holiday reduced total volume by approximately 1.0% in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025.

Net sales increased 5.4% to $2.0 billion in the third quarter of 2026. The growth in net sales was primarily the result of annual pricing actions with additional contribution from higher volumes. Sparkling and Still net sales increased 4.4% and 6.8%, respectively, in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025. The increase in Sparkling category net sales was driven primarily by sales of multi-pack, take-home aluminum can packages sold within our large store, club and value channels. Net sales of our Still products were especially strong in our convenience and value store channels. Volume mix in Still products contributed to the revenue growth as Energy and Protein products made up more of the sales growth in the quarter.

Gross profit in the third quarter of 2026 was $771.6 million, an increase of $23.1 million, or 3.1%. On an adjusted(b) basis, gross profit increased $20.4 million, or 2.7%. Gross margin in the third quarter of 2026 decreased 80 basis points to 38.8%. Adjusted(b) gross margin in the third quarter of 2026 decreased 100 basis points to 38.6%. The annual price increases we executed earlier this year across our portfolio have helped to offset a portion of the higher commodity and supply chain costs experienced during the third quarter of 2026. However, the growth in certain supply chain and commodity costs, in particular aluminum and fuel, has outpaced our price increases, leading to the gross margin compression during the quarter. Additionally, our gross margin for the third quarter of 2026 was negatively impacted by the shift in our sales mix. For the first


nine months of 2026, gross profit increased $159.0 million, or 7.5%. For the first nine months of 2026, adjusted(b) gross profit increased $109.4 million, or 5.2%.

"We are pleased with the continued momentum of our business and the success of our commercial strategies, which drove strong net sales growth in the quarter," said Dave Katz, President and Chief Operating Officer. "Higher aluminum costs, a challenging transportation market and increased fuel costs continued to pressure our gross margin. However, disciplined expense management, along with cycling our prior-year investment in our teammates, allowed us to leverage our operating expenses and expand our operating margin. Together with The Coca-Cola Company, we remain focused on affordability as we invest strategically with our customers to drive balanced, durable growth.”

Selling, delivery and administrative (“SD&A”) expenses in the third quarter of 2026 decreased $1.9 million, or 0.4%. SD&A expenses as a percentage of net sales decreased to 25.1% in the third quarter of 2026 from 26.6% in the third quarter of 2025. The change in SD&A expenses was driven by an increase in labor costs related to annual wage adjustments and higher employee benefit costs, offset by a favorable, non-cash fair value adjustment to our fuel hedging positions of approximately $6 million. On an adjusted(b) basis, SD&A expenses in the third quarter of 2026 increased by $3.8 million, or 0.8%. On an adjusted(b) basis, SD&A expenses as a percentage of net sales declined to 25.4% in the third quarter of 2026 from 26.6% in the third quarter of 2025. SD&A expenses in the first nine months of 2026 increased $87.0 million, or 6.2%. The increase in SD&A expenses in the first nine months of 2026 was driven by an additional investment in the base wages of our front-line teammates, which became effective at the beginning of the third quarter of 2025. Approximately $25.0 million of the year-to-date increase was related to the six additional days in the first nine months of 2026.

Income from operations in the third quarter of 2026 was $271.6 million, compared to $246.6 million in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of $25.0 million, or 10.1%. On an adjusted(b) basis, income from operations in the third quarter of 2026 was $262.9 million, an increase of $16.6 million, or 6.7%. Operating margin for the third quarter of 2026 was 13.7% as compared to 13.1% for the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 60 basis points. Adjusted(b) operating margin for the third quarter of 2026 was 13.2% as compared to 13.0% for the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 20 basis points. For the first nine months of 2026, income from operations increased $71.9 million, or 10.1%. On an adjusted(b) basis, income from operations in the first nine months of 2026 increased $35.6 million, or 5.0%. The six additional selling days in the first nine months of 2026 accounted for approximately $30.0 million of the increase in income from operations.

Net income in the third quarter of 2026 was $175.9 million, compared to $142.3 million in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of $33.5 million, or 23.6%. On an adjusted(b) basis, net income in the third quarter of 2026 was $175.3 million, compared to $179.2 million in the third quarter of 2025, a decrease of $3.9 million, or 2.2%.


Net income in the third quarter of 2025 was adversely impacted by non-cash, fair value adjustments to our acquisition related contingent consideration as compared to the third quarter of 2026.

Net income for the first nine months of 2026 was $446.2 million, compared to $433.3 million in the first nine months of 2025, an improvement of $12.9 million, or 3.0%. On an adjusted(b) basis, net income in the first nine months of 2026 was $482.5 million, compared to $510.6 million in the first nine months of 2025, a decrease of $28.2 million, or 5.5%. Net income for the first nine months of 2026 was also negatively impacted by an increase in net interest expense as compared to the first nine months of 2025. Net income in the first nine months of 2026 benefited from the impact of six additional days, which increased net income by approximately $22.6 million during the period.

Cash flows from operations for the first nine months of 2026 were $664.8 million, compared to $722.9 million for the first nine months of 2025. During the first nine months of 2026, we invested approximately $219 million in capital expenditures. In fiscal year 2026, we expect capital expenditures to be approximately $300 million.

As a reminder, the first quarter of 2026 included six extra days as compared to the first quarter of 2025. The fourth quarter of 2026 will include six fewer days as compared to the fourth quarter of 2025. The full fiscal years of 2026 and 2025 have the same number of days.

(a) All comparisons are to the corresponding period in the prior year unless specified otherwise.
(b) The discussion of the operating results for the third quarter ended October 2, 2026 and the first nine months of 2026 includes selected non-GAAP financial information, such as “adjusted” results. The schedules in this news release reconcile such non-GAAP financial measures to the most directly comparable GAAP financial measures.

About Coca-Cola Consolidated, Inc.
Headquartered in Charlotte, N.C., Coca‑Cola Consolidated (NASDAQ: COKE) is the largest Coca‑Cola bottler in the United States. We make, sell and distribute beverages of The Coca‑Cola Company, and other partner companies, in more than 300 brands and flavors across 14 states and the District of Columbia, to approximately 60 million consumers.



For over 124 years, we have been deeply committed to the consumers, customers and communities we serve and passionate about the broad portfolio of beverages and services we offer. Our Purpose is to honor God in all we do, to serve others, to pursue excellence and to grow profitably.

More information about the Company is available at www.cokeconsolidated.com. Follow Coca‑Cola Consolidated on Facebook, X, Instagram and LinkedIn.
