D.R. HORTON, INC., AMERICA’S BUILDER, REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER AND FISCAL 2026 EARNINGS AND INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND TO $0.50 PER SHARE

ARLINGTON, Texas (Business Wire) - October 29, 2026 - D.R. Horton, Inc. (NYSE:DHI), America’s Builder, today reported fourth quarter and fiscal 2026 results. All comparisons in this release are to the respective prior year period, unless noted otherwise.

Fiscal 2026 Fourth Quarter Highlights
•Net income attributable to D.R. Horton of $741.8 million or $2.67 per diluted share
•Consolidated pre-tax income of $1.0 billion, with a pre-tax profit margin of 10.8%
•Consolidated revenues of $9.4 billion
•Home sales revenues of $8.4 billion on 22,915 homes closed
Fiscal 2026 Highlights
•Net income attributable to D.R. Horton of $2.9 billion or $10.11 per diluted share
•Consolidated pre-tax income of $3.9 billion, with a pre-tax profit margin of 11.8%
•Consolidated revenues of $33.0 billion
•Home sales revenues of $30.6 billion on 84,202 homes closed
•Cash provided by operations totaled $3.5 billion
•Debt to total capital of 20.8%
•Book value per share increased 4% to $85.14
•Repurchased 24.0 million shares of common stock for $3.6 billion, reducing outstanding share count by 8%, and paid cash dividends of $513.3 million
•Return on equity was 12.2% and return on assets was 8.2%

Consolidated Results
Three months ended September 30, 2026
Net income attributable to D.R. Horton for its fourth fiscal quarter decreased 18% to $741.8 million, and earnings per diluted share decreased 12% to $2.67. Consolidated pre-tax income was $1.0 billion on revenues of $9.4 billion, resulting in a pre-tax profit margin of 10.8%. Results for the quarter include $91.4 million of inventory impairment charges to cost of sales in the Company’s rental segment.



Fiscal year ended September 30, 2026
For the fiscal year, net income attributable to D.R. Horton decreased 19% to $2.9 billion, and earnings per diluted share decreased 13% to $10.11. Consolidated pre-tax income was $3.9 billion on revenues of $33.0 billion, resulting in a pre-tax profit margin of 11.8%.

Cash provided by operations was $3.5 billion in fiscal 2026 and consisted primarily of $2.7 billion, $289.5 million, $271.1 million and $148.6 million of cash provided by the homebuilding, Forestar, financial services and rental segments. Total liquidity at year end was $7.2 billion. The Company repaid $600 million principal amount of its 1.3% homebuilding senior notes at maturity in October 2026 and has no other senior note maturities in fiscal 2027. At September 30, 2026, the Company's debt to total capital ratio was 20.8%. Debt to total capital ratio consists of notes payable divided by stockholders' equity plus notes payable.

In fiscal 2026, the Company's return on equity (ROE) was 12.2%, and return on assets (ROA) was 8.2%. ROE is calculated as net income attributable to D.R. Horton for the year divided by average stockholders' equity, where average stockholders' equity is the sum of ending stockholders' equity balances of the trailing five quarters divided by five. ROA is calculated as net income attributable to D.R. Horton for the year divided by average consolidated assets, where average consolidated assets is the sum of total asset balances for the trailing five quarters divided by five.

David Auld, Executive Chairman, said:
"The D.R. Horton team completed our 25th consecutive year as America's largest builder by volume, and we are proud to have provided homeownership to more than 84,000 individuals and families during fiscal 2026, including approximately 43,500 first-time homebuyers. Consolidated pre-tax income for the year totaled $3.9 billion on $33.0 billion of revenues, resulting in a pre-tax profit margin of 11.8%. We generated $3.5 billion of cash from operations in fiscal 2026 and returned $4.1 billion to shareholders through repurchases and dividends while maintaining a strong financial position.

"Housing market conditions became more challenging during the fourth quarter as rising mortgage rates, affordability constraints and cautious consumer sentiment continued to pressure new home demand. Our experienced operators responded with discipline by adjusting incentives, pricing and starts on a community-by-community basis to align our inventory levels with current demand. We expect incentives to remain elevated in fiscal 2027, the extent of which will depend on market conditions throughout the year.

"Our strong liquidity, low leverage, local market expertise and national scale provide us with significant financial and operational flexibility to adapt to changing market conditions and deliver attractive shareholder returns. Over the past 5 years, we have made significant investments in our housing platform as we entered 30 new markets and 7 new states and increased our community count by 65%. Over that same period, book value per share grew at a 15% compound annual rate, while we generated $14.0 billion of operating cash flow and returned all of it to shareholders through repurchases and dividends, reflecting our consistent execution, operational efficiency and disciplined, balanced capital allocation.

"Based on our solid financial position and cash flow, our Board recently increased our quarterly cash dividend by 11% to $0.50 per share, making fiscal 2027 our thirteenth consecutive year of dividend growth. We will continue to focus on delivering value to our homebuyers, gaining market share and generating compelling returns for our shareholders over the long term."

Homebuilding
Three months ended September 30, 2026
Homebuilding revenue for the fourth quarter decreased 2% to $8.4 billion, and homes closed decreased 2% to 22,915 homes. Homebuilding pre-tax income decreased 11% to $891.0 million, and pre-tax profit margin was 10.6%. Net sales orders decreased 7% to 18,619 homes and 9% in value to $6.6 billion. The Company’s cancellation rate (cancelled sales orders divided by gross sales orders) was 23% compared to 20% in the prior year quarter.

Fiscal year ended September 30, 2026
Homebuilding revenue for the fiscal year decreased 3% to $30.7 billion, and homes closed decreased 1% to 84,202 homes. Homebuilding pre-tax income decreased 17% to $3.4 billion, and pre-tax profit margin was 11.2%. Net sales orders increased 2% to 84,995 homes and were unchanged in value at $30.9 billion. The cancellation rate was 19% compared to 18% in the prior year.

At fiscal year end, the Company had 28,200 homes in inventory, of which 17,700 were unsold. 8,800 of the Company’s unsold homes were completed, including 600 that had been completed for greater than six months. Of the Company’s homes closed during fiscal 2026, 68% were on lots developed by Forestar or third parties, up from 65% during fiscal 2025.



The Company’s homebuilding return on inventory (ROI) for fiscal 2026 was 16.6%. Homebuilding ROI is calculated as homebuilding pre-tax income for the year divided by average inventory, where average inventory is the sum of ending homebuilding inventory balances for the trailing five quarters divided by five.

Non-Homebuilding Segments
Three months ended September 30, 2026
•Rental: Rental revenues were $647.1 million with a pre-tax loss of $25.3 million, which includes $91.4 million of inventory impairment charges to cost of sales related to nine multi-family rental projects. The Company's rental operations sold 509 single-family rental homes for $134.3 million and 2,182 multi-family rental units for $512.8 million.
•Forestar: Forestar sold 5,004 lots and generated $580.8 million of revenues and $78.6 million of pre-tax income, resulting in a pre-tax profit margin of 13.5%.
•Financial Services: Financial services revenues were $219.2 million with pre-tax income of $78.2 million, resulting in a pre-tax profit margin of 35.7%.
Fiscal year ended September 30, 2026
•Rental: Rental revenues were $1.2 billion with pre-tax income of $18.2 million, which includes $91.4 million of inventory impairment charges to cost of sales. The Company's rental operations sold 2,073 single-family rental homes for $560.0 million and 2,737 multi-family rental units for $666.5 million. At year end, the consolidated balance sheet included $291.3 million of single-family rental property inventory and $2.3 billion of multi-family rental property inventory.
•Forestar: Forestar sold 13,545 lots and generated $1.6 billion of revenues and $192.1 million of pre-tax income, resulting in a pre-tax profit margin of 11.7%.
•Financial Services: Financial services revenues were $817.3 million with pre-tax income of $258.1 million, resulting in a pre-tax profit margin of 31.6%.

Share Repurchases and Dividends
During the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, the Company repurchased 9.4 million shares of common stock for $1.4 billion, for a total of 24.0 million shares repurchased for $3.6 billion during the year. Common shares outstanding at September 30, 2026 totaled 271.4 million, down 8% from a year ago, and the remaining stock repurchase authorization was $4.7 billion.

The Company paid cash dividends of $125.0 million during the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, for a total of $513.3 million of dividends paid during the year. Subsequent to year-end, the Company declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.50 per share, an increase of 11% from its most recent dividend paid. The dividend is payable on November 19, 2026 to stockholders of record on November 12, 2026.

About D.R. Horton, Inc.
D.R. Horton, Inc., America’s Builder, has closed more than 1.3 million homes in its 48-year history and has been the largest homebuilder in the United States by volume since 2002. D.R. Horton has operations in 128 markets in 38 states across the United States and is engaged in the construction and sale of high-quality homes through its diverse product portfolio with sales prices generally ranging from $200,000 to $700,000. The Company also constructs and sells both single-family and multi-family rental properties. During its fiscal year ended September 30, 2026, D.R. Horton closed 84,202 homes in its homebuilding operations, in addition to 2,073 single-family rental homes and 2,737 multi-family rental units in its rental operations. D.R. Horton also provides mortgage financing, title services and insurance agency services for its homebuyers and is the majority-owner of Forestar Group Inc., a publicly traded national residential lot development company.
