Park National Corporation reports financial results
for third quarter and first nine months of 2026

NEWARK, Ohio ‒ Park National Corporation (Park) (NYSE American: PRK) today reported financial results for the third quarter and the first nine months of 2026. Park's board of directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $1.10 per common share and a special cash dividend of $1.30 per common share, payable on December 10, 2026, to common shareholders of record as of November 20, 2026.

Park’s net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $56.9 million, a 20.7 percent increase from $47.2 million for the third quarter of 2025. The third quarter of 2026 included $7.0 million ($5.5 million after tax) in expenses related to the merger with First Citizens Bancshares, Inc. Third quarter 2026 net income per diluted common share was $3.13, compared to $2.92 for the third quarter of 2025. Park's net income for the first nine months of 2026 was $157.4 million, a 14.5 percent increase from $137.4 million for the first nine months of 2025. The first nine months of 2026 included $26.5 million ($21.0 million after tax) in merger-related expenses and other income reducing charges. Net income per diluted common share for the first nine months of 2026 was $8.77, compared to $8.48 for the first nine months of 2025.

“Our continued solid financial performance reflects the strength of our relationship-based approach to banking and the dedication of our colleagues who work every day to help things go right for our stakeholders,” said Park CEO and president Matthew R. Miller. “We are grateful for the trust our customers and communities place in us, including the communities we have recently welcomed in Tennessee.

“We are once again pleased to recognize the commitment of our shareholders with a special dividend payable in December. Their continued support enables us to advance our mission of helping those we serve achieve financial success and prosperity.”

Park’s total loans increased $1.72 billion, or 21.4 percent, during 2026. The increase to total loans included $1.58 billion in loans acquired through the First Citizens transaction. Park's total deposits increased $2.38 billion, or 28.9 percent, during 2026, with an increase of 27.3 percent including off balance sheet deposits. The increase in total deposits included $2.22 billion in deposits acquired through the First Citizens transaction. The combination of solid loan growth and steady deposits contributed to Park's success in 2026.

“Our results represent the compounded effect of our colleagues' best efforts every day,” said Park Chairman David L. Trautman. “From our newest team members to those who have dedicated more than 45 years to Park, our people pour themselves into serving others, trusting that if we do the right things consistently, the results will follow. So far this year, the scoreboard looks favorable.”

Headquartered in Newark, Ohio, Park National Corporation has $12.6 billion in total assets (as of September 30, 2026). Park's banking operations are conducted through its subsidiary, The Park National Bank. Other Park subsidiaries are Scope Leasing, Inc. (d.b.a. Scope Aircraft Finance), Park Investments, Inc., Park National Holdings, Inc., First Citizens Properties, Inc., First Citizens Risk Management, Inc., and SE Property Holdings, LLC.
