PowerCompute (Nasdaq: PWCM) Increases October Bitcoin Production 20% Year-Over-Year as Fleet Refresh Lifts Active Hashrate 11.4%

 

Mined 9.0 Bitcoin in October 2026, up 11.1% from September 2025; active hashrate reached approximately 800 PH/s at October 31, up 11.4% from approximately 718 PH/s at September 30, 2026

 

Generated approximately $46,000 from energy sales in October 2026, held 65.7 Bitcoin at October 31, 2026, approximately $2.00 per share

 

TAMPA, FL, November 10, 2026 - PowerCompute, Inc. (Nasdaq: PWCM) (“PowerCompute” or the “Company”), formerly LM Funding America, Inc., a Bitcoin mining and power-infrastructure company developing high-performance computing (“HPC”) and artificial intelligence (“AI”) infrastructure, today announced preliminary, unaudited Bitcoin mining and operating results for the month ended October 31, 2026. The Company mined approximately 9.0 Bitcoin in October 2026, up 20% from 7.5 Bitcoin in October 2025 and up 11.1% from 8.1 Bitcoin in September 2026. Active hashrate reached approximately 800 PH/s at October 31, 2026, up 11.4% from approximately 718 PH/s at September 30, 2026, as the Company installed approximately 900 more efficient miners on its existing 26 megawatts of power.

October 2026 Highlights

•
Energy sales: Generated approximately $46,000 in October 2026 by selling energy at its Oklahoma site during periods of economic curtailment, when the value of power sold to the grid exceeded the value of mining with it.
•
Bitcoin held: 65.7 BTC at October 31, 2026, valued at approximately $5.1 million based on a Bitcoin price of approximately $77,000 on that date, or approximately $2.00 per share of common stock outstanding.

“We are putting more hashrate on the same power,” said Bruce M. Rodgers, Chairman, Chief Executive Officer and President of PowerCompute. “Active hashrate rose about 11.1% in October 2026 without adding a megawatt, and our production is starting to reflect it. We are funding the refresh from Bitcoin and cash on hand, not new debt. Getting more Bitcoin out of every kilowatt-hour is the point of the refresh, and we’ll keep replacing older machines where the economics support it.”

 

The Company had 2,535,061 shares of common stock outstanding as of October 31, 2026.

About PowerCompute

PowerCompute, Inc. (Nasdaq: PWCM), formerly LM Funding America, Inc., is an owner and operator of electrical infrastructure that converts electricity into Bitcoin and high-performance computing and artificial intelligence capacity. Founded in 2008 and headquartered in Tampa, Florida, the Company operates 26 megawatts of interconnected electrical infrastructure across Bitcoin data center facilities in Oklahoma and Mississippi.

The Company also operates a technology-enabled specialty finance business providing funding to nonprofit community associations primarily in Florida. For more information, please visit https://www.power-compute.com.
