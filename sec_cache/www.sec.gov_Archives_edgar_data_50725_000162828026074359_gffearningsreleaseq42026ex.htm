Griffon Corporation Announces Annual and Fourth Quarter Results

NEW YORK, NEW YORK, November 17, 2026 – Griffon Corporation (“Griffon” or the “Company”) (NYSE:GFF) today reported results for the fourth quarter and fiscal year ended September 30, 2026.

Revenue for fiscal 2026 totaled $1.85 billion, a 3% increase compared to $1.80 billion in the prior year, primarily driven by favorable residential and commercial price and mix of 6%, partially offset by decreased volume of 3% driven by residential.

Fiscal 2026 income from continuing operations totaled $235.6 million, or $5.19 per share, compared to $20.2 million, or $0.43 per share in the prior year. Excluding all items that affect comparability from both periods, adjusted income from continuing operations (a non-GAAP measure) was $245.7 million, or $5.42 per share, in the current year compared to $238.9 million, or $5.12 per share, in the prior year. For a reconciliation of income from continuing operations to adjusted income from continuing operations (a non-GAAP measure), and earnings per share from continuing operations to adjusted earnings per share from continuing operations (a non-GAAP measure), see the attached table.

Fiscal 2026 adjusted EBITDA from continuing operations of $459.6 million was consistent with the prior year of $461.8 million, reflecting the increased revenue noted above offset by the unfavorable impact of decreased volume on overhead absorption, and increased material, labor and distribution costs. For a definition of adjusted EBITDA and a reconciliation of income from continuing operations to adjusted EBITDA (a non-GAAP measure), see the attached table.

Revenue for the fourth quarter totaled $490.9 million, a 3% increase compared to $475.6 million in the prior year quarter, primarily due to favorable residential and commercial price and mix of 6%, partially offset by decreased volume of 3% driven by both residential and commercial.

Income from continuing operations in the fourth quarter totaled $66.8 million, or $1.49 per share, compared to $20.0 million, or $0.43 per share, in the prior year quarter. Excluding all items that affect comparability from both periods, adjusted income from continuing operations was $72.0 million, or $1.61 per share, in the current year quarter compared to $65.2 million, or $1.42 per share, in the prior year quarter.
Adjusted EBITDA from continuing operations for the fourth quarter was $127.8 million, a 4% increase from the prior year quarter of $122.9 million, driven by the increased revenue noted above, partially offset by the unfavorable impact of decreased volume on overhead absorption, and increased material, labor and distribution costs.

“Fiscal 2026 was a transformational year for Griffon,” said Ronald J. Kramer, Chairman and CEO of Griffon. “We completed a comprehensive series of strategic actions that repositioned Griffon into a pure play building products company, and significantly strengthened our balance sheet by extending our bond maturities to 2034 and reducing our debt by $364 million. Griffon is well-positioned for the future, and we expect our growth to accelerate as building activity improves.”

“Our solid operational and financial results for the year were highlighted by strong free cash flow of $267 million. This, together with our strategic actions, enabled us to repurchase 1.6 million, or 3.5%, of Griffon’s shares outstanding while reducing leverage to 2.0x from 2.4x.”

“We will continue to use our operating cash flow to support our capital allocation strategy with a focus on opportunistically repurchasing shares, reducing debt, and funding our regular quarterly dividend, while investing in the growth of our businesses. Reinforcing this strategy, earlier today we announced a $400 million increase in our share repurchase authorization, and a 14% increase in our regular quarterly dividend to $0.25 per share. These actions reflect the strength of our business and our confidence in our strategic plan and outlook,” Mr. Kramer stated in conclusion.

Taxes
For the years ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, the Company reported pre-tax income from continuing operations and recognized an effective tax rate of 28.2% and 75.7%, respectively. Excluding discrete and certain other tax provisions, net, and items that affect comparability, the effective tax rates for the years ended September 30, 2026 and 2025 were 27.8% and 27.2%, respectively.

Balance Sheet and Capital Expenditures
As of September 30, 2026, the Company had cash and equivalents of $98.6 million and total debt outstanding of $1.0 billion, resulting in net debt of $0.9 billion. During fiscal 2026, debt was reduced by approximately $363.8 million. Leverage, as calculated in accordance with our credit agreement (see the attached table), was 2.0x net debt to EBITDA as of September 30, 2026 compared to 2.4x as of September 30, 2025. At September 30, 2026, borrowing availability under the revolving credit facility was $225.6 million, subject to certain loan covenants. Free cash flow from continuing operations was $267.0 million and capital expenditures, net, were $37.9 million for year ended September 30, 2026. For a definition of free cash flow from continuing operations (a non-GAAP measure) and a reconciliation of net cash provided by operating activities from continuing operations to free cash flow from continuing operations, see the attached table.

Share Repurchases

Share repurchases during the quarter ended September 30, 2026 totaled 0.3 million shares of common stock, for a total of $32.1 million, or an average of $93.11 per share. Share repurchases totaled 1.6 million shares of common stock in fiscal 2026, for a total of $136.3 million, or an average of $83.14 per share.

During the period October 1, 2026 through market close on November 16, 2026, Griffon repurchased 0.2 million shares of common stock for a total of $16.9 million or an average of $96.22 per share. Since April 2023 and through November 16, 2026, share repurchases totaled 12.6 million shares of common stock, or 22.1% of the outstanding shares, for a total of $713.2 million or an average of $56.48 per share.

As announced earlier today, Griffon's Board of Directors authorized the repurchase of an additional $400 million of shares of Griffon common stock. As of November 17, 2026, inclusive of this increase, $544.8 million remained under the Board authorized share repurchase program.

2027 Outlook

Griffon expects fiscal 2027 revenue and adjusted EBITDA to be consistent with the prior year at $1.85 billion and $460 million, respectively. Free cash flow, including capital expenditures of $60.0 million, is expected to approximate income from continuing operations, with depreciation of $26.0 million and amortization of $15.0 million. Fiscal year 2027 interest expense is expected to be $50.0 million, inclusive of interest income from notes receivable. Griffon’s normalized tax rate is expected to be 28%.

About Griffon Corporation

Griffon Corporation is a leading provider of residential and commercial building products. The Company is the largest North American manufacturer and marketer of garage doors under the Clopay, IDEAL and Holmes brands, and rolling steel door and grille products under the Clopay, Cornell, and Cookson brands. The Company is also a leading provider of residential, industrial, and commercial ceiling fans sold under the Hunter, Casablanca, and Jan Fan brands.

For more information on Griffon, please see the Company’s website at www.griffon.com.
