New Fortress Energy Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results
NEW YORK, Nov. 16, 2026 – New Fortress Energy Inc. (Nasdaq: NFE) (“NFE” or the “Company”) today reported its financial results for the third quarter of 2026.
“With our restructuring complete, we are now a transformed company with significantly reduced debt and a simplified, capital-light business plan. Our power assets are in great demand and the worldwide need for LNG continues to be robust. As we execute on our business plan, we are focused on leveraging our assets to generate strong returns and positive cash flow from operations,” said Wes Edens, Chief Executive Officer of NFE.
Third Quarter Highlights
•On Sept. 11, 2026, NFE completed its restructuring and recapitalization under the UK Restructuring Plan. As part of the restructuring transaction:
•Debt principal was reduced from approximately $5.7 billion to approximately $0.7 billion.(1)
•In exchange for cancellation of certain debt interests, creditors received NFE’s Brazil business, NFE Class A common stock, and preferred stock and preferred interests with a liquidation preference of $2.45 billion and $0.2 billion, respectively.
•NFE effected a 1-for-50 reverse stock split of its Class A common stock.
•Total revenues were $335.8 million in the third quarter of 2026, up $109.7 million from the second quarter of 2026, primarily from higher cargo sales, $39.4 million of contract novation revenue, and higher volumes sold in Mexico.
•Net income was $863.4 million in the third quarter of 2026, driven by:
•Non-cash gains of $1.6 billion on the restructuring and extinguishment of debt, partly offset by
•$467.9 million of non-cash asset impairments, $160.8 million of interest expense and $47.3 million of transaction and integration costs related to the restructuring
•Adjusted EBITDA(2) was $48.4 million for the quarter, driven by higher cargo sales margin and $39.4 million of contract novation revenue, partly offset by higher operating and maintenance and turbine lease costs.
•Cash and cash equivalents were $270.5 million as of Sept. 30, 2026.

Recent Developments
•FLNG 1: As announced in October 2026, FLNG 1 is offline following a mechanical issue with the facility’s turbine which powers the compressor. The liquefaction equipment itself was not affected. Before the outage, FLNG 1 was online approximately 98% of the time in 2026, excluding scheduled maintenance. Based on the most recent discussions with the turbine manufacturer, NFE expects the turbine to be ready November 23rd. Testing and installation are then expected to take several weeks, and NFE continues to expect FLNG 1 to return to service in the fourth quarter of 2026. As disclosed in its Form 10-Q, because the timing of FLNG 1’s return to service is outside the Company’s control, there is substantial doubt about NFE’s ability to continue as a going concern. In addition to returning FLNG 1 to service, the Company continues to evaluate other actions to improve its liquidity and cash flows.
•Turbine lease: In October 2026, NFE agreed to lease ten mobile turbines, capable of generating up to 300 MW, to a U.S.-based investment-grade company for an initial term of five years. Deliveries are expected to begin in the fourth quarter of 2026 and be completed in the first quarter of 2027. The lease is expected to be accretive to earnings and improve NFE’s liquidity.

About New Fortress Energy Inc.
New Fortress Energy Inc. (Nasdaq: NFE) is a global energy infrastructure company founded to address energy poverty and accelerate the world’s transition to reliable, affordable, and clean energy. The Company owns and operates natural gas and liquefied natural gas (LNG) infrastructure and an integrated fleet of ships and logistics assets to rapidly deliver turnkey energy solutions to global markets. Collectively, the Company’s assets and operations reinforce global energy security, enable economic growth, enhance environmental stewardship and transform local industries and communities around the world.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
Consolidated Segment Operating Margin and Adjusted EBITDA are non-GAAP measures. Management uses them to evaluate operating performance, and we believe investors use them to measure operating performance without regard to items such as interest expense, taxes, depreciation and amortization, which vary substantially from company to company depending on capital structure, the method by which assets were acquired and depreciation policies. Their principal limitation is that they exclude significant expenses and income required by GAAP. They should not be considered in isolation or as substitutes for net income or other GAAP measures, and may not be comparable to similarly titled measures of other companies or to measures used for covenant compliance. Investors should review the reconciliation to net income and not rely on any single financial measure.
Consolidated Segment Operating Margin is defined as net (loss) income, adjusted for selling, general and administrative expenses, transaction and integration costs, depreciation and amortization, asset impairment expenses, loss (gain) on sale, loss on extinguishment of debt, net, gain on restructuring, interest expense, other (income) expense, net, and tax (benefit) provision. Adjusted EBITDA is Consolidated Segment Operating Margin less Core SG&A, plus depreciation included in cost of sales. Core SG&A is total SG&A excluding non-cash share-based compensation, non-capitalizable development expenses, costs to pursue new business opportunities, and non-recurring litigation provisions, which we exclude because they are not indicative of ongoing operating performance.
Beginning in the third quarter of 2026, Adjusted EBITDA excludes discontinued operations and the gains on restructuring and extinguishment of debt from the Restructuring Transaction.
