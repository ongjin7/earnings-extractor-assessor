Management Commentary
“The third quarter represented another meaningful step forward in the financial performance of our business,” said J. Bryan Kitchen, President and Chief Executive Officer of Ascent Industries Co. “Net sales from Continuing Operations increased 37.8% year over year to $27.1 million, gross profit increased 21.9% to $7.1 million and Adjusted EBITDA increased 155.8% to $3.5 million, amid muted market conditions. Sequentially, net sales increased 5.7%, gross profit increased 28.3% and Adjusted EBITDA improved by approximately $2.1 million. For the trailing twelve months ended September 30, 2026, the Company achieved post-COVID record net sales of $91.0 million across Chemicals and post-COVID record Adjusted EBITDA of $2.9 million across continuing operations.”

“We are encouraged by the progress reflected in our third-quarter results,” Kitchen added. “As we work to grow our way out of the built-in seasonality associated with our legacy business, we recognize that there is additional work ahead to make that improvement more consistent and durable. As we continue to scale the business, our focus is increasingly on the operating disciplines that support sustainable margin expansion — improving how work moves through the organization, standardizing processes across our assets, increasing automation where appropriate and addressing areas of structural cost. Pricing has contributed to performance, but over time we expect a greater share of margin improvement to come from better execution, productivity and a more efficient operating model.”

“The performance of Midwest Graphic Sales continues to reinforce our confidence in both the acquisition and the growth runway ahead,” Kitchen continued. “In its first full quarter as part of Ascent, Midwest contributed positively to our financial results and remained accretive to earnings when excluding the impact of corporate allocations. The manufacturing transition remains on schedule, and our teams continue to identify and progress commercial opportunities beyond the original underwriting case.”

“We are also pleased to report that the Company received the full $4.55 million escrow holdback associated with the sale of certain assets related to Bristol Metals, further strengthening our liquidity,” Kitchen said. “Separately, our business generated

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Net sales from continuing operations were $27.1 million compared to $19.7 million in the third quarter of 2025. Excluding Midwest, net sales from continuing operations were $24.9 million in the third quarter of 2026. The increase was a result of increases in volume and average selling prices.

Gross profit from continuing operations increased 21.9% to $7.1 million, or 26.2% of net sales, compared to $5.8 million, or 29.7% of net sales, in the third quarter of 2025. Excluding Midwest, gross profit from continuing operations increased 14.1% to $6.7 million in the third quarter of 2026. The increase in dollars was primarily driven by increases in sales and cost recovery in the period due to increased production partially offset by increases in material cost, labor and overhead and shipping costs.

Net income from continuing operations increased to $1.4 million compared to a net loss of ($0.1) million in the third quarter of 2025. Diluted earnings per share increased to $0.15 in the third quarter of 2026 compared to a diluted loss per share of ($0.01) in the third quarter of 2025. Excluding Midwest, net income from continuing operations increased to $2.1 million and diluted earnings per share increased to $0.23 in the third quarter of 2026.

Adjusted EBITDA from continuing operations increased to $3.5 million in the third quarter of 2026, with adjusted EBITDA margin increasing to 13.0% compared to 7.0% in the prior year period. Excluding Midwest, Adjusted EBITDA from continuing operations increased to $3.8 million, with adjusted EBITDA margin increasing to 15.1% in the third quarter of 2026. The increase was primarily driven by the aforementioned increase in gross profit as well as reductions in SG&A in the current year.

Liquidity
As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $28.7 million in cash and cash equivalents, no debt outstanding under its revolving credit facilities and had $21.1 million in availability under its revolving credit facility.

For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, the Company repurchased no shares under its authorized share repurchase program. Year to date, the Company has repurchased 505,563 shares at an average cost of $13.29 per share for approximately $6.8 million.
