AMCON DISTRIBUTING COMPANY REPORTS RESULTS FOR THE FISCAL YEAR ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2026

 

NEWS RELEASE

 

Omaha, NE, November 9, 2026 - AMCON Distributing Company (“AMCON” or “the Company”) (NYSE American: DIT, NYSE Texas: DIT), an Omaha, Nebraska based Convenience and Foodservice Distributor, is pleased to announce fully diluted earnings per share of $2.52 on net income available to common shareholders of $2.4 million for the fiscal year ended September 30, 2026.

 

“AMCON’s long-term strategy of providing superior customer service, custom designed merchandising platforms supported by advertising and leading-edge technology solutions combined with the third largest Convenience Distribution footprint in the United States positions our organization well in the years ahead,” said Christopher H. Atayan, AMCON’s Chairman and Chief Executive Officer. He further noted, “We continue to actively seek strategic acquisition opportunities for operators who want to align with our customer-focused approach and further the legacy of their enterprises.”

 

The wholesale distribution segment reported revenues of $3.1 billion and operating income of $25.5 million for fiscal 2026 and the retail health food segment reported revenues of $43.7 million and an operating loss of $0.6 million for fiscal 2026.

 

“This summer our management team navigated a monumental logistics challenge presented by wildfires that engulfed a large portion of our service territory in Colorado. Through the hard work and dedication of our associates, we were able to maintain service levels and timely deliveries for our customers across this region. When we speak to AMCON’s service philosophy, it is events like this that reinforce our key philosophies and value-add to our customers,” said Andrew C. Plummer, AMCON’s President and Chief Operating Officer. Mr. Plummer continued, “AMCON’s proprietary technology suite of services, combined with our integrated state of the art advertising, design, print and electronic display programs, are designed to provide our customers a competitive edge. Our customer base and our management team work collaboratively to tailor unique solutions as we deploy these capabilities across our entire organization. As we grow, this ability to deliver customized programs is a competitive differentiation.”

 

“We engage in a relentless daily focus on managing the Company’s balance sheet and maximizing our liquidity position. At September 30, 2026, our shareholders’ equity was $117.0 million,” said Charles J. Schmaderer, AMCON’s Chief Financial Officer. Mr. Schmaderer also added, “We have a long-term commitment to investing in technology-based business process improvements to optimize our ability to serve our growing base of customers. We believe these investments enable our customers to compete more effectively in the markets they serve.”

 

AMCON, and its subsidiaries Team Sledd, LLC and Henry’s Foods, Inc., is a leading Convenience and Foodservice Distributor of consumer products, including beverages, candy, tobacco, groceries, foodservice, frozen and refrigerated foods, automotive supplies and health and beauty care products serving thirty-five (35) states from thirteen (13) distribution centers in Colorado, Idaho, Illinois, Indiana, Minnesota, Missouri, Nebraska, North Dakota, Ohio, South Dakota, Tennessee and West Virginia. Through its Healthy Edge Retail Group, AMCON operates fifteen (15) health and natural product retail stores in the Midwest and Florida.
