Presurance Holdings Reports 2026 Third Quarter Financial Results

Troy, MI, November 12, 2026 – Presurance Holdings, Inc. (Nasdaq: PRHI) (“Presurance” or
the “Company”) today reported financial results for the third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

Third Quarter & YTD 2026 Financial Highlights

•
Personal Lines Business Continues to Generate Profitable Underwriting Results

Combined ratio for Q3 2026 improved to 82.7% from 95.2% in the prior year period.

YTD 2026 combined ratio of 91.0%; a significant improvement from 119.3% for the first nine months of 2025.
•
Improved YTD Overall Underwriting Performance

Overall underwriting combined ratio improved to 97.1% for the nine months ending September 30, 2026, compared to 133.7% in the prior-year period.
•
Book value is now $6.45 per share as of September 30, 2026.

 

Management Comments

“During the third quarter, we continued to execute our overall strategic priorities while addressing the remaining obligations associated with our legacy portfolio,” said Brian Roney, Chief Executive Officer of Presurance Holdings, Inc. “We are pleased to report lessening legacy impacts, as our ongoing core Personal Lines business continues to perform profitably.”

2026 Third Quarter Gross Written Premium

Gross written premiums decreased 57.8% to $6.2 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $14.6 million in the third quarter of 2025. For the nine months ending September 30, 2026, gross written premiums decreased 40.8% to $30.7 million, compared to $51.9 million in the prior-year period.

The reduction in premium volume reflects the Company's continued emphasis on underwriting discipline, and selective risk acceptance in potentially softening market conditions. The Company remains focused on its personal lines homeowners business while continuing to manage its legacy Commercial Lines portfolio in runoff.

Certain reserve strengthening in the third quarter of 2026 was largely due to development experienced solely in the legacy run-off book of business.

Definitions of Non-GAAP Measures

Presurance prepares its public financial statements in conformity with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America (GAAP). Statutory data is prepared in accordance with statutory accounting rules as defined by the National Association of Insurance Commissioners' (NAIC) Accounting Practices and Procedures Manual and therefore is not reconciled to GAAP data.

We believe that investors’ understanding of the Company’s performance is enhanced by our disclosure of adjusted operating income. Our method of calculating this measure may differ from that used by other companies and therefore comparability may be limited. We define adjusted operating income (loss), a non-GAAP measure, as net income (loss) excluding: 1) net realized investment gains (losses), 2) change in fair value of equity securities, 3) change in fair value of contingent considerations, 4) contingent consideration bonus expense and 5) additional accretion of warrants from Series B Preferred Stock payoff. We use adjusted operating income as an internal performance measure in the management of our operations because we believe it gives our management and other users of our financial information useful insight into the results of our operations and underlying business performance.
