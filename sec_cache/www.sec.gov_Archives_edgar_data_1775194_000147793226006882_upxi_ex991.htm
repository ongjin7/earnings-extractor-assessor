EXHIBIT 99.1


Upexi Reports Fiscal First Quarter 2027 Financial Results

 

Earnings per Diluted Share Totaled $0.68 for the Quarter

Digital Asset Revenue Totaled $3.1 Million

Solana Treasury Totaled Approximately 2.35 Million SOL as of September 30, 2026

 

TAMPA, Fla., Nov. 10, 2026 -- Upexi, Inc. (NASDAQ: UPXI) (the “Company” or “Upexi”), a leading Solana-focused digital asset treasury company and consumer brands owner, today announced its financial results for the fiscal first quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

Allan Marshall, Chief Executive Officer of Upexi, stated, “During the first quarter of fiscal 2027, we strengthened our balance sheet by increasing our Solana treasury to approximately 2.35 million SOL, increasing our cash on hand to $6.4 million, and increasing our stockholders’ equity to $49.2 million. Additionally, in September we successfully reduced the interest rate on our BitGo borrowings from 11.5% to 7.5%, a reduction we expect to lower annual interest expense by approximately $2.3 million.”

 

Financial Highlights for the Quarter Ended September 30, 2026

•

Total revenue for the quarter was approximately $3.5 million, compared to $9.2 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. Product revenue was $0.4 million, compared to $3.2 million, reflecting the Company’s shift away from legacy health, wellness and other consumer-product lines and the exit of certain manufacturing and distribution operations.

•

Digital asset revenue, which primarily consists of staking income, totaled $3.1 million, compared to $6.1 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.  The decline was primarily due to lower SOL token prices year over year, as the number of tokens earned as staking revenue was approximately flat compared to the year ago quarter.

•

General and administrative expenses were $7.1 million, compared to $9.3 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025, a decrease of approximately 24%.

•

Net income totaled $89.1 million, or $0.97 per basic share and $0.68 per diluted share, compared to net income of $66.7 million, or $1.21 per basic share and $0.76 per diluted share, for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. Net income was largely driven by $102.0 million of unrealized gains on digital assets, reflecting non-cash quarter-end fair value adjustments.

•

Cash totaled $6.4 million as of September 30, 2026, up approximately 11% from June 30, 2026.

•

Total stockholders’ equity was $49.2 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to negative $53.8 million as of June 30, 2026.

•

As of November 10, 2026, the Company had 88,855,392 shares of common stock outstanding.

 

Solana Treasury

•

Total SOL held of approximately 2.35 million as of September 30, 2026, with an average cost of approximately $154 per SOL.

•

Over 95% of SOL held was staked as of September 30, 2026.

•

Earned approximately 31,300 SOL of digital asset revenue during the quarter.

•

As of November 10, 2026, the Company held approximately 2,360,265 SOL.

Operational and Other Highlights During and Subsequent to the Quarter

•

Amended the Company’s credit facility with BitGo Prime, LLC, reducing the interest rate from 11.5% to 7.5% per year and lowering the required collateral. Based on the $57.3 million outstanding as of September 30, 2026, the Company expects the reduction to lower annual interest expense by approximately $2.3 million.

•

Issued approximately 8.8 million shares of common stock under the Company’s at-the-market program during the quarter for approximately $10.1 million of gross proceeds.

•

Purchased approximately 30,100 SOL for approximately $3.5 million and sold approximately 53,000 SOL, including SOL received as staking revenue, for proceeds of approximately $4.6 million during the quarter.

 

The Company’s Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for this period is available on the SEC’s website and on Upexi’s IR website.

 

About Upexi, Inc.

Upexi, Inc. (Nasdaq: UPXI) is a leading digital asset treasury company, where it aims to acquire and hold as much Solana (SOL) as possible in a disciplined and accretive fashion. In addition to benefiting from the potential price appreciation of Solana - the cryptocurrency of the leading high-performance blockchain - Upexi utilizes three key value accrual mechanisms in intelligent capital issuance, staking, and discounted locked token purchases. The Company operates in a risk-prudent fashion to position itself for any market environment and to appeal to investors of all kinds, and it currently holds over 2.3 million SOL. Upexi also continues to be a brand owner specializing in the development, manufacturing, and distribution of consumer products. Please see www.upexi.com for more information.
