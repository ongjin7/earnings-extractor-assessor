Kulicke & Soffa Reports Fourth Quarter 2026 Results

Singapore – November 18, 2026 – Kulicke and Soffa Industries, Inc. (NASDAQ: KLIC) (“Kulicke & Soffa,” “K&S,” “our,” or the “Company”), today announced financial results of its fourth fiscal quarter ended October 3, 2026. The Company reported fourth quarter net revenue of $410.3 million, net income of $73.2 million, representing EPS of $1.37 per fully diluted share, and non-GAAP net income of $81.4 million, representing non-GAAP EPS of $1.52 per fully diluted share.

Raj Talluri, Kulicke & Soffa’s President and Chief Executive Officer, stated, “I look forward to bringing greater focus and leading the Company through its next phase of growth. This has been a tremendous quarter for many reasons – we delivered strong financial results, core wire bonding demand is resilient, our technology is evolving with AI, Data Center and Advanced Packaging trends, and we have additional visibility within our growing Advanced Solutions portfolio."
Kulicke & Soffa initiated Advanced Solutions system manufacturing within its newly completed Singapore production floor during the fiscal first quarter 2027.

About Kulicke & Soffa
Kulicke & Soffa is a global leader in semiconductor assembly technology, advancing device performance across automotive, compute, industrial, memory and communications markets. Founded on innovation in 1951, K&S is uniquely positioned to overcome increasingly dynamic process challenges – creating and delivering long-term value by aligning technology with opportunity.
