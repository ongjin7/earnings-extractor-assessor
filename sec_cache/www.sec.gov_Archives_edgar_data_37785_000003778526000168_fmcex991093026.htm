PHILADELPHIA, October 29, 2026 –FMC Corporation (NYSE:FMC) today reported third quarter 2026 revenue of $106 million, compared with $542 million in the prior-year period. Third-quarter sales include a $639 million negative impact from the commercial actions announced on October 15, 2026, to modify the company’s pricing and rebate model in North America and to address select higher-priced products held by channel customers in key markets.

Commercial Actions Update
The commercial actions announced on October 15 resulted in a total third-quarter negative impact of approximately $639 million to revenue and $672 million negative impact to Adjusted EBITDA. These actions were taken to address the elevated value of FMC inventory held by channel customers in key markets as they seek to operate with very low inventory values in response to high financing costs, the perception of ample supply and demand uncertainty. This creates a specific constraint for FMC, driven by two distinct issues: the existing price and rebate model in North America and select products already in the channel at prices above current market levels.
Approximately $500 million of the Adjusted EBITDA impact relates to the transition to a new pricing and rebate model in North America, which is intended to significantly reduce the gap between gross and net pricing. This amount consists of two components.
The first and larger component reflects the pricing and rebate adjustments associated with the transition to the new model. Under the previous model, rebates for the market year ended September 30 were paid in the fourth quarter, offset in part by continued fourth-quarter sales at the higher gross price. With the new model effective October 1, that cycle will not continue. As a result, the remaining adjustment to close out the historical model is reflected in third-quarter results.
The second component reflects adjustments needed to bring inventory already in the North America channel from the historic higher gross price to a price closer to the expected net price under the new model. FMC expects the new model to materially reduce the volatility associated with future rebate

The remaining approximately $175 million of the impact reflects pricing adjustments made on high-priced products held by channel customers in Brazil, the United States, Canada and Argentina. These pricing adjustments are intended to support movement of the affected products downstream and future demand across the FMC portfolio. The adjustments apply only to products already held by channel customers and do not change FMC's current selling prices. The adjustments do not impact current period cash and are the only component of the Adjusted EBITDA charge expected to reduce future cash collections.
The estimated third-quarter financial impact of both actions is reflected in the company’s reported results; however, implementation is ongoing. Discussions with customers are underway, and FMC expects the transition to occur over the next three quarters.

Outlook1
Fourth-quarter revenue, excluding India, is expected to be in the range of $1.04 billion to $1.14 billion, essentially flat at the midpoint versus fourth-quarter 2025. A high-single digit price decline is expected to be offset by volume growth driven by increased direct-to-grower sales in Brazil, growth of new products and more competitive pricing of FMC products.
Adjusted EBITDA is forecasted to be in the range of $210 million to $230 million, a decline of 22 percent at the midpoint versus the prior-year period. Higher volume and cost favorability are expected to be more than offset by lower price and an FX headwind. FMC expects adjusted earnings per diluted share to be in the range of $0.56 to $0.70, a decline of 48 percent at the midpoint versus fourth-quarter 2025, mainly due to lower EBITDA and an increased share count following the investment from Tessenderlo Group which closed in September.

About FMC
FMC Corporation is a global agricultural sciences company dedicated to helping growers produce food, feed, fiber and fuel for an expanding world population while adapting to a changing environment. FMC's innovative crop protection solutions – including biologicals, crop nutrition, digital and precision agriculture – enable growers and crop advisers to address their toughest challenges economically while protecting the environment. FMC is committed to discovering new herbicide, insecticide and fungicide active ingredients, product formulations and pioneering technologies that are consistently better for the planet.
