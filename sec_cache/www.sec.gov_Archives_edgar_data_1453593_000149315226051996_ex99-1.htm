Xtant Medical Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

and Announces Board-led Strategic Review Process

 

Board reiterates commitment to improving performance and maximizing stockholder value; has been actively involved in a comprehensive review of a broad range of strategic, operational, and financial alternatives.

 

BELGRADE, Mont., November 16, 2026 — Xtant Medical Holdings, Inc. (NYSE American: XTNT), a medical technology company focused on surgical solutions for spinal and other orthopedic conditions, today reported financial and operating results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights

 

●Total revenue was $24.3 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $33.3 million for the third quarter of 2025 and up 5% from $23.0 million in the second quarter of 2026. The year-over-year decrease primarily reflects the absence of revenue from divested businesses and wound care licensing revenue.

 

●Biologics revenue was $19.3 million for the third quarter of 2026, up 13% from the third quarter of 2025 and up 11% from the second quarter of 2026, third quarter 2026 revenue includes $2.2 million from sales of HEMOBLAST® Bellows.

 

“The Board is disappointed with Xtant’s financial performance this quarter, which marks the second consecutive quarter in which our results have not met our guidance. That is unacceptable,” said Stavros Vizirgianakis, Chairman of the Board. “Certain directors, including me, have invested their own capital in Xtant, and the Board includes a representative of our largest stockholder. The Board feels this shortfall as owners and stewards of the Company, not just as directors, and the Board’s interests are directly aligned with stockholders. The Board’s priorities are clear: improve execution, improve financial performance, and maximize stockholder value through the strategic review process. Already we are encouraged by how our core biologics business is gaining momentum both quarter-over-quarter and year-over-year.”

 

The Board’s active strategic review process is evaluating a broad range of potential actions to maximize stockholder value, which may include, among other things, operational and performance improvement initiatives, capital allocation and capital structure alternatives, commercial or strategic partnerships, restructurings, financings, acquisitions, dispositions, business combinations or other transactions. The Board has not set a timetable for completion of the strategic review process, and there can be no assurance that the process will result in any particular action or outcome. The Company does not intend to disclose further developments unless and until the Board approves a specific action or otherwise determines that further disclosure is appropriate or required.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

Revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $24.3 million compared to $33.3 million for the third quarter of 2025 and up 5% from $23.0 million in the second quarter of 2026. The year-over-year decrease primarily reflects the absence of $5.4 million of prior-year revenue from the divested non-core Coflex/CoFix assets and international hardware business, and the absence of $5.5 million of prior-year wound care licensing revenue, partially offset by $2.2 million of HEMOBLAST revenue. Biologics revenue was $19.3 million, an increase of 13% from $17.1 million for the third quarter of 2025 and 11% from $17.4 million for the second quarter of 2026. Biologics product revenue excluding HEMOBLAST and products affected by the market for wound care reimbursement, a non-GAAP measure, grew 8% compared to the second quarter of 2026 and 6% compared to the third quarter of 2025.

 

 

 

 

Gross margin for the third quarter of 2026 was 56.3%, compared to 66.1% for the same period in 2025. The decrease primarily reflected a 670-basis-point impact from the cessation of high-margin wound care licensing revenue and a 360-basis-point impact from sales mix.

 

Operating expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $16.7 million, compared to $19.5 million for the third quarter of 2025. General and administrative expenses decreased to $5.0 million from $7.1 million, due primarily to the divested non-core Coflex/CoFix assets and international hardware business and lower expense under compensation plans. Sales and marketing expenses decreased to $11.2 million from $11.7 million due primarily to savings related to the divestitures and lower consulting fees, partially offset by higher compensation expense from increased sales headcount, including sales personnel hired in connection with the Company’s exclusive U.S. distribution agreement for HEMOBLAST, and by higher independent agent commissions and travel expenses.

 

Net loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $3.6 million, or $0.03 per share, compared to net income of $1.3 million, or $0.01 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2025.

 

Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP measure, was a loss of $1.5 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to adjusted EBITDA of $4.5 million for the third quarter of 2025.

 

The Company defines adjusted EBITDA as net income/loss from operations before depreciation, amortization and interest income/expense and provision for income tax/benefit, and as further adjusted to add back in or exclude, as applicable, non-cash compensation, the write-off of the distribution agreement deposit, disposition/ acquisition-related income and expenses, acquisition-related fair value adjustments, unrealized foreign currency translation gain or loss, and separation-related expenses. A calculation and reconciliation of adjusted EBITDA to net income (loss) is included in the financial tables at the end of this release.

 

As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $7.8 million of cash and cash equivalents; $22 million of total indebtedness, consisting of $10.2 million of current and long-term debt and $11.8 million of borrowings under its revolving credit facility; and $2.1 million of availability under its revolving credit facility. As of December 31, 2025, the Company had $17.1 million of cash and cash equivalents; $25.4 million of total indebtedness, consisting of $14.5 million of current and long-term debt and $10.9 million of revolving credit borrowings; and $3.8 million of revolving credit availability.

 

Based on its current operating plan, the Company expects its cash and cash equivalents, and availability under its revolving credit facility, to be sufficient to fund operations for at least the next 12 months.

 

 

 

 

2026 Financial Guidance

 

Based on year-to-date performance, the Company no longer expects to achieve its previously issued full-year 2026 guidance. While the Board of Directors conducts its strategic review process, the Company is not providing revised full-year 2026 financial guidance. At the same time, as detailed elsewhere in this release, the Board is encouraged by the recent momentum in the Company’s core biologics business and remains committed to scaling this franchise throughout the strategic review process.

About Xtant Medical Holdings, Inc.
