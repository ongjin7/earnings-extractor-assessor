Cognizant Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
12% year-over-year revenue growth in Financial Services; strong EPS growth, adjusted operating margin expansion, and free cash flow generation
•Revenue of $5.7 billion increased 4.6% year-over-year or 4.8% in constant currency1
•Operating margin of 15.4% decreased 60 basis points year-over-year; Adjusted Operating Margin1 of 16.3% increased 30 basis points year-over-year
•GAAP EPS2 of $1.39 increased 148% year-over-year; Adjusted EPS1 of $1.49 increased 7.2% year-over-year
•Trailing 12-month bookings of $28.7 billion increased 4% year-over-year
•2026 constant currency revenue growth guidance is revised to 4.0% to 4.4% year-over-year growth
•2026 Adjusted Operating Margin guidance is now approximately 16.1%, year-over-year expansion of 30 basis points
•2026 Adjusted Diluted EPS guidance revised to $5.72 to $5.79, year-over-year growth of 8% to 10%
TEANECK, N.J., October 29, 2026 - Cognizant (Nasdaq: CTSH), a leading AI builder and technology services provider, today announced its third quarter 2026 financial results.

“We delivered third-quarter revenue above the midpoint of our guidance range, including another quarter of double-digit growth in Financial Services and our seventh consecutive quarter of year-over-year adjusted operating margin expansion,” said Ravi Kumar S, Chief Executive Officer. “The AI market is moving from capability to value realization, requiring enterprises to reimagine how work gets done, build AI into the fabric of the enterprise and create new sources of value. We are making that transition alongside our clients, with over 50% of our code now AI-assisted, over 95,000 frontier AI badges and certifications earned by our associates and continued improvement in revenue and adjusted operating income per employee. What we learn as we apply AI across Cognizant strengthens how we partner with clients through our AI Builder strategy, helping them make AI a new production function that creates enterprise value at scale.”

“Our third-quarter performance reflects the strength of our operating model and disciplined approach to capital allocation,” said Jatin Dalal, Chief Financial Officer. “Adjusted EPS grew 7%, exceeding revenue growth both in the quarter and year-to-date. Through the first nine months of 2026, we have expanded adjusted operating margin by 30 basis points, generated $1.6 billion of free cash flow and deployed $2.3 billion on share repurchases and dividends. We achieved these results while investing in strategic partnerships and AI skilling initiatives, and integrating recently completed acquisitions. We believe this balanced approach supports sustainable growth and long-term value creation.”
Bookings
On a trailing-twelve-month basis, bookings increased 4% year-over-year to $28.7 billion, which represented a book-to-bill of approximately 1.3x. Bookings in the third quarter declined 7% year-over-year. Third quarter bookings included three large deals, which are deals with total contract value of $100 million or greater.
Employee Metrics
On a trailing-twelve months basis, Voluntary Attrition - Tech Services was 12.6% for the period ended September 30, 2026, as compared to 13.0% and 12.6% for the periods ended June 30, 2026 and September 30, 2025, respectively. Total headcount as of September 30, 2026 was 359,900, an increase of 3,200 from June 30, 2026 and an increase of 10,100 from September 30, 2025.
Capital Allocation
The Company repurchased 4.9 million shares for $258 million during the third quarter under its share repurchase program. As of September 30, 2026, there was $2.1 billion remaining under the share repurchase authorization. In October 2026, the Company declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.33 per share for shareholders of record on November 13, 2026. This dividend will be payable on November 25, 2026.
Project Leap
In the second quarter of 2026, we introduced Project Leap, a program designed to accelerate our transformation to the operating model of the future by funding investments in our integrated offerings, AI capabilities and partnerships, reshaping productivity through competitive offerings and upskilling our workforce. During the third quarter of 2026, we revised our estimates related to Project Leap. We now expect to record total costs of $180 million to $250 million, with $140 million to $160 million of costs expected to be incurred in 2026 and the remainder to be incurred through the first quarter of 2027. Cash payments related to the costs are expected to be made primarily over the same period. This consists of $150 million to $200 million of employee severance and other personnel related costs and $30 million to $50 million of other charges. This program is expected to generate in-year savings of approximately $200 million in 2026, which are and will continue to be used primarily to fund investments as described above.

Select Company, Client and Partnership Announcements
Cognizant is building a portfolio of capabilities combined with deep domain expertise to harness and advance an AI-led future. Cognizant’s progress has been accelerated through client agreements, platform enhancements, partnerships, and investments in AI skilling. Recent announcements include:
Client Announcements
•Announced a five-year extension of its work with Gilead Sciences, a biopharmaceutical company. Under the agreement, Cognizant will support Gilead's continued development of DevOps capabilities and the responsible application of agentic AI tools across selected technology programs. The agreement follows more than a decade of work in which Cognizant has supported elements of Gilead's global IT infrastructure, platforms, applications and advanced analytics.
•Selected by the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development (EBRD) to support its Tech Blueprint, the Bank’s technology transformation program, which focuses on driving efficiency, effectiveness and innovation through digitally enabled processes powered by AI, data, and intelligent automation. Under the five-year framework, Cognizant aims to deliver critical technology services across two complementary service models: outcome-led managed services and fixed-capacity services, spanning digital and workplace management, cloud infrastructure, data and security, software engineering, automation and AI.
•Announced the next phase of its strategic partnership with Travelport, an intelligent infrastructure travel provider, marking a new phase focused on embedding AI across the platforms, products and operations that power the travel industry. Since announcing their AI transformation in May 2026 alongside Anthropic, Cognizant and Travelport have completed the first phase of the initiative, establishing the foundational platforms, architecture and modern engineering practices required for AI-enabled delivery.
•Selected by SITA, a leading specialist in air transport technology, as the implementation and transformation partner for SITA's SKY program. The groupwide, finance-led initiative will help SITA implement a modern cloud platform across finance, procurement and supply chain processes.
•Announced production results from autonomous AI engineering work at Odyssey Logistics, a global logistics provider, along with an expanded set of collaborative offerings built on Cognition's AI software engineer, Devin. Odyssey is working to launch its OdysseyONE platform, intended to support order intake, execution, visibility and lifecycle management across its network. Odyssey set out to consolidate its order intake and full shipment lifecycle onto that platform. A seven-person Cognizant team used Devin to handle the legacy conversion. Delivery throughput rose by roughly a third and Odyssey recorded a 37 percent net cost saving against the conventional approach.
•Announced the successful implementation and go-live of Cognizant's Application Management Services (AMS) at Axis Bank, one of the largest private sector banks in India, for the bank's AMS 2.0 initiative. AMS 2.0 is a structured program designed to strengthen delivery governance, improve operational consistency and accelerate the adoption of automation across application support services.
•Selected to partner with Benchling, a leading AI platform for biotech R&D, to accelerate R&D at research sites for Kyowa Kirin, a Japan-based global specialty pharmaceutical company. Benchling gives scientists a single place to capture structured data, run AI models and agents and automate the workflows that move discovery and development forward. Through Cognizant's implementation support, researchers in the Research Division of Kyowa Kirin will be able to efficiently and seamlessly conduct a range of research activities, from experiment planning and execution to data collection and use of AI, within a single environment.



•Chosen by Ingenico, a global provider of in-person commerce solutions and services, to help simplify and modernize Ingenico's global technology operations. Under the five-year agreement, Cognizant has been tasked with embedding AI across Ingenico's infrastructure and operations, security and data to support the modernization of Ingenico's technology operations, including infrastructure, security, business and corporate applications and data systems, while providing managed services across end-user services, service desk, application support and engineering.
Platform Enhancements and Partnerships
•Announced the general availability of Workflow Agentic Processing and Enterprise Model Context Protocol (MCP) Servers for the TriZetto Facets® and QNXT™ core administration platforms. The Workflow Agentic Processing capability lets AI agents carry out a health plan's standard operating procedures to automate processing of eligible routine pending claims, while denials, exceptions and complex cases are routed to human reviewers when additional judgment is needed.
•Expanded its partnership with Google Cloud by establishing a new Gemini Frontier Capability Group backed by 18,000 dedicated Cognizant associates. The new group pairs Google's full-stack, enterprise-ready AI platform with Cognizant's domain, industry and engineering expertise as an AI Builder.
•Announced an expanded collaboration with CrowdStrike, a global cybersecurity company, to introduce Cognizant Cybersecurity for Operational Technology, an Operational Technology (OT) security service powered by CrowdStrike Falcon® for XIoT. The service combines Cognizant's industrial engineering expertise and managed security operations with CrowdStrike's unified visibility and protection across XIoT and IT environments designed to help protect mission-critical assets.

•Entered into a definitive agreement to acquire DyFlex Solutions, a leading SAP transformation firm. Cognizant AI Nexus is designed to help enterprises build compounding value from their most effective processes. Cognizant Activate is expected to deliver platform-aligned, pre-configured solution kits spanning data and AI, cybersecurity, enterprise applications, cloud modernization and managed services.
•Detailed its expanded commitment to America's workforce. •Completed its Americas Codex hackathon, a hybrid event held with OpenAI in which more than 2,600 associates built solutions using OpenAI Codex. The event is a step forward in Cognizant's frontier-certified workforce skilling strategy and its broader investment in American AI-era jobs. The event followed Cognizant’s 10,000-participant flagship Codex Hackathon in India in late July.
•Launched its Talent Accelerator program with 489 newly hired graduates from 58 business schools across 37 cities in India. The program is a two-week immersive onboarding held simultaneously across Cognizant's facilities in Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Pune, designed to help new hires learn about the company and its values. It also combines functional, sales and marketing training, alongside direct interaction with Cognizant's business and leadership teams.

About Non-GAAP Financial Measures and Performance Metrics

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
To supplement our financial results presented in accordance with GAAP, this press release includes references to the following measures defined by the Securities and Exchange Commission as non-GAAP financial measures: Adjusted Operating Margin, Adjusted Income from Operations, Adjusted Net Income, Adjusted Diluted EPS (or Adjusted EPS), free cash flow and constant currency revenue growth. These non-GAAP financial measures are not based on any comprehensive set of accounting rules or principles and should not be considered a substitute for, or superior to, financial measures calculated in accordance with GAAP, and may be different from non-GAAP financial measures used by other companies. In addition, these non-GAAP financial measures should be read in conjunction with our financial statements prepared in accordance with GAAP. The reconciliations of our non-GAAP financial measures to the corresponding GAAP measures should be carefully evaluated.
Our non-GAAP financial measures Adjusted Operating Margin and Adjusted Income from Operations exclude unusual items, such as Project Leap charges and the partial reversal of the India Defined Contribution Obligation in 2026 and the gain on sale of property and equipment in 2025. Our non-GAAP financial measures Adjusted Net Income and Adjusted Diluted EPS exclude unusual items, such as Project Leap charges, the partial reversal of the India Defined Contribution Obligation, the gain on sale of property and equipment and the gain on sale of property and equipment and the one-time income tax expense related to the enactment of the OBBBA, net non-operating foreign currency exchange gains or losses and the tax impact of all the applicable adjustments. The income tax impact of each item excluded from Adjusted Net Income and Adjusted Diluted EPS is calculated by applying the statutory rate and local tax regulations in the jurisdiction in which the item was incurred. Free cash flow is defined as cash flows from operating activities plus proceeds from sale of property and equipment, net of purchases of property and equipment. Constant currency revenue growth is defined as revenues for a given period restated at the comparative period’s foreign currency exchange rates measured against the comparative period's reported revenues.
Management believes providing investors with an operating view consistent with how we manage the Company provides enhanced transparency into our operating results. For our internal management reporting and budgeting purposes, we use various GAAP and non-GAAP financial measures for financial and operational decision-making, to evaluate period-to-period comparisons, to determine portions of the compensation for our executive officers and for making comparisons of our operating results to those of our competitors. Accordingly, we believe that the presentation of our non-GAAP measures, which exclude certain costs, when read in conjunction with our reported GAAP results, can provide useful supplemental information to our management and investors regarding financial and business trends relating to our financial condition and results of operations.
A limitation of using non-GAAP financial measures versus financial measures calculated in accordance with GAAP is that non-GAAP financial measures do not reflect all of the amounts associated with our operating results as determined in accordance with GAAP and may exclude costs that are recurring such as our net non-operating foreign currency exchange gains or losses. In addition, other companies may calculate non-GAAP financial measures differently than us, thereby limiting the usefulness of these non-GAAP financial measures as a comparative tool. We compensate for these limitations by providing specific information regarding the GAAP amounts excluded from our non-GAAP financial measures to allow investors to evaluate such non-GAAP financial measures.



Performance Metrics
Bookings are defined as total contract value (or TCV) of new contracts, including new contract sales as well as renewals and expansions of existing contracts. Bookings can vary significantly quarter to quarter depending in part on the timing of the signing of a small number of large contracts. Our book-to-bill ratio is defined as bookings for the trailing twelve months divided by revenue for the same period. Measuring bookings involves the use of estimates and judgments and there are no independent standards or requirements governing the calculation of bookings. The extent and timing of conversion of bookings to revenues may be impacted by, among other factors, the types of services and solutions sold, contract duration, the pace of client spending, actual volumes of services delivered as compared to the volumes anticipated at the time of sale, and contract modifications, including terminations, over the lifetime of a contract. The majority of our contracts are terminable by the client on short notice often without penalty, and some without notice. We do not update our bookings for subsequent terminations, reductions or foreign currency exchange rate fluctuations. Information regarding our bookings is not comparable to, nor should it be substituted for, an analysis of our reported revenues. However, management believes that it is a key indicator of potential future revenues and provides a useful indicator of the volume of our business over time. Large deals and mega deals are defined as deals with a total contract value of $100 million or greater and $500 million or greater, respectively.
